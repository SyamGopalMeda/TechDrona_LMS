# Tech Drona Can Win on Verifiable Trust

Tech Drona's best opening is not more content or more surveillance. It is the trust problems its reference platforms leave unsolved, and it should fix them with standards-grade engineering. The Indian placement-prep incumbents all organise content the same way: Quant, Logical and Verbal aptitude, then programming, DSA, CS core and company hubs. They sell "anchor price plus coupon" subscriptions. Yet the public complaint record against the market leader centres on denied refunds, missing courses, unresponsive support and buffering video ([ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/bycompany/prepinsta-a488452.html)). None of the platforms examined publishes a device limit, a concurrent-session rule or proof that it can detect account sharing. PrepInsta's Terms ban sharing but state no technical limit ([PrepInsta Terms](https://prepinsta.com/terms-and-conditions/)). Only Testbook was found to tie simultaneous multi-device use to account blocking, and that page has since gone 404 ([Testbook Help](https://intercom.help/testbook-da1e94f5d0c5/en/articles/8123166-account-blocking)). The engineering blueprint is mature and public. Moodle shows how to build a crash-safe quiz engine, context-scoped roles and a concurrent-login limit. HackerRank and Judge0 show how to run hidden-test coding judges and advisory integrity reports. NIST SP 800-63B-4 and OWASP ASVS 5.0 give exact numbers for passwords, hashing and session timeouts. The binding constraint is Indian law. The DPDP Act's substantive duties start around **13 May 2027** ([dpdprules.org](https://dpdprules.org/blog/dpdp-18-months-to-12-months-proposal)), and they offer **no "legitimate interests" basis** for fraud detection ([s.7](https://dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-2/section7.html)). They also **ban tracking and behavioural monitoring of under-18 users** ([DPDP Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). So Tech Drona's anti-sharing system has to be deterministic, disclosed, appealable and minor-aware, not a black-box fingerprint score. The recommended stance is threefold. Copy proven mechanics: a server-authoritative timer, autosave, hidden test cases, `__Host-` session cookies and Argon2id hashing. Enforce a published Adobe-style device and stream policy at the video-token layer. Differentiate on honest pricing, TPO (training and placement officer) readiness dashboards, verifiable certificates and transparent integrity review.

> **How to read the evidence labels.** **[Verified]** = stated on a public primary page, statute, standard or source code, cited. **[Reported]** = observed behaviour, secondary or press reporting, user complaints, or vendor claims that nobody has validated independently. **[Rec]** = a recommendation or assumption by this report, not a sourced fact. Prices, legal dates and vendor figures change, so re-verify them before you commit to anything. Nothing here is legal advice.

Moodle has none of these natively. **[Rec]**

## 3. Account security: nobody publicly proves they stop sharing

### What the public record establishes

The verified record is thin and almost entirely contractual. Coursera requires users to "create, access, and/or use only one user account" and not "share access to your account or access information" ([Coursera Terms](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms)). Unacademy calls the subscription "of a personal nature", not to be "transferred to or shared with any other person for consideration or otherwise", and says nothing about devices ([Unacademy Terms](https://unacademy.com/terms)). PrepInsta calls purchases "non-transferable and … for the sole use of the buyer only" and threatens permanent suspension ([PrepInsta Terms](https://prepinsta.com/terms-and-conditions/)). Skillshare requires one person per account ([ConductAtlas](https://conductatlas.com/platform/skillshare/skillshare-terms-of-service/account-must-be-used-by-one-person-only/)). A different edtech company, prepedu.com (not PrepInsta), reserves suspension for "login attempts exceeding the permitted number of devices" but never states that number ([prepedu T&C](https://prepedu.com/en/terms-and-conditions-of-transactions)). **[Verified]**

Technical behaviour is known mostly from reports. Physics Wallah's app reportedly logs out the first device when a second logs in, while web and app can run at the same time ([Primebook](https://www.primebook.in/blog/how-to-use-physics-wallah-app-on-two-devices)). Unacademy reportedly allowed "only two web login sessions" ([The Ken](https://the-ken.com/tag/adda-247/)). Both claims are **[Reported]**, and the Unacademy one is more than six years old.

Outside edtech, the documented schemes all pair detection with a legitimate remedy. Netflix determines a household "based on IP address, device IDs, and account activity" and states "we do not collect GPS data" ([Netflix Help](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/124925)). The widely reported "every 31 days" rule was retracted as posted in error ([Sound & Vision](https://www.soundandvision.com/content/what-you-need-do-netflix-account-sharing-crackdown)). Disney+ uses an email one-time passcode for "away from home" ([OTTVerse](https://ottverse.com/disney-launches-paid-sharing-plan-to-tackle-password-sharing/)). Spotify Family uses address re-verification. A member who fails moves to the free tier and is barred from other Family plans for 12 months, rather than being deleted ([Spotify Support](https://support.spotify.com/ca-en/article/family-address-verification/)). Adobe allows sign-in on two computers but active use on one, and asks the user to sign out of a device when activating a third ([U. Michigan KB](https://teamdynamix.umich.edu/TDClient/30/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=14132)) **[Reported, institutional source]**.

### Strengths and weaknesses of each detection signal

The signals differ sharply in reliability, and Indian network conditions degrade the IP-based ones most. Cloudflare reports that one IPv4 address "may represent hundreds or even thousands of users". User-to-IP ratios are highest in parts of Africa and South Asia. CGNAT IPs get rate-limited about 3x more often despite bot rates nearly identical to other IPs, at 4.8% vs 4.7% ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/detecting-cgn-to-reduce-collateral-damage/)). **[Verified]**

| Signal | Strengths | Weaknesses (evidence) | Recommended weight **[Rec]** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Concurrent authenticated sessions or streams** (server-side) | A server-side fact with no ambiguity. Moodle implements it ([session manager](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/lib/classes/session/manager.php)) | Kills unsaved work and conflicts with SSO (Moodle's own warning) ([auth.php](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/lang/en/auth.php)). One legitimate user with a phone and a laptop trips it | **Primary enforcement point**, especially at video-token issuance and assessment start |
| **First-party device token** (random ID in a `__Host-` cookie or app keystore) | Cheap, stable until cleared, easy to explain in a "your devices" page | Users can clear it, and incognito resets it. No external accuracy data | **Primary device counter** |
| **IP address** | Distinguishes the same Wi-Fi from a different network; shows burst patterns | CGNAT on Indian mobile and many residential ISPs ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/detecting-cgn-to-reduce-collateral-damage/); [Airtel CGNAT](https://anuragbhatia.com/2014/10/networking/airtel-3g-running-cgnat/); [TechEnclave](https://techenclave.com/t/act-private-nat-anyone-ever-got-away-from-it/421392?page=2)). | **Impossible travel** | Catches a stolen credential used from a distant city | Microsoft needs a learning period ("the earliest of 14 days or 10 logins") and suppresses VPN false positives [Reported] ([Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/768911/atypical-travel-unfamiliar-sign-in-properties)). | **Dynamic watermark (name or masked phone)** | Deters handing over the account and traces leaks. "DRM blocks downloading, and the watermark deters and traces" [Reported, vendor] ([VdoCipher](https://www.vdocipher.com/dynamic-watermarking/)) | An iframe embed cannot show the user ID ([VdoCipher](https://vdocipher.com/subtitle/)). No independent evidence of effectiveness against phone-camera recording | High deterrent value at low cost |

The synthesis is that **the strongest sharing evidence is behavioural overlap**: two or more distinct device tokens with overlapping playback or test sessions, from distinct networks, repeated over days. No single network signal comes close. **[Rec]** This matters legally as well as technically. DPDP s.8(3) requires that personal data used to make a decision affecting a person be complete, accurate and consistent ([DPDP Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). A lockout triggered by a shared college NAT IP fails that standard on its face. **[Rec]**

### Recommended Tech Drona device and sharing policy [Rec]

Publish the numbers in the Terms and FAQ, which is exactly what no incumbent does. A defensible starting policy has five numbers. Up to **3 registered devices**. **2 concurrent signed-in sessions**. **1 concurrent video stream**, enforced by refusing a new DRM playback token while one is active. **1 session during any assessment.** **2 self-service device resets per 30 days.** Enforcement follows a graduated ladder that mirrors the remedy-first pattern of Netflix, Disney+ and Spotify. It starts with a soft prompt ("You're logged in on another device: continue here?"). Next comes a forced logout of the older session, then a step-up OTP to the registered phone, then a temporary lock after repeated high-confidence overlap. Suspension comes only after a human reviews the logged signals and the user has had an in-app appeal. A paid "extra learner" or buddy seat turns sharers into revenue, following the paid-sharing logic of Netflix and Disney+ ([Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/disney-plus-password-sharing-crackdown-begins-paid-sharing-1236011760/)). For confirmed minors, enforce only the deterministic caps on sessions, streams and device counts. Do not build behavioural risk profiles of minors until counsel signs off (see section 8).

## 5. Admin and trainer workflows: scoped roles beat Moodle's four-state permissions

Moodle's model, roles made of capabilities assigned in a context with Allow, Prevent and Prohibit overrides, is powerful ([role.php](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/lang/en/role.php)). It is also hard enough to debug that Moodle ships dedicated "Check permissions" tooling ([docs](https://docs.moodle.org/en/Check_permissions)). Tech Drona's nine roles map cleanly onto a thinner model: **allow-only permissions, assigned at a scope** of platform, organisation (college), batch, course or item, with deny by default. **[Rec]**

| Tech Drona role | Moodle analogue | Scope | Core workflows | Auth and session tier **[Rec]** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Individual student | Student (self or fee enrolment) | Own data | Learn, practise, test, book mentors, manage devices and billing | MFA optional (offered); 60 min idle / 24 h absolute |
| College-enrolled student | Student via cohort sync | Own data; batch visible to coordinators | Same, plus batch drives and coordinator-assigned tests | Same; college may require MFA |
| College coordinator (TPO) | Manager at category | Their college's batches only | Roster CSV import, seat assign and revoke, batch creation, assign tests and drives, readiness dashboards, export reports | MFA required; 30 min idle / 12 h absolute |
| Trainer | Editing teacher at course | Assigned batches and courses | Author and schedule tests, grade, grant overrides (extra time), review integrity flags | MFA required; 30 min / 12 h |
| Content manager | Course creator plus question-bank owner | Catalogue | Question-bank authoring, versioning, review workflow, completeness tracking, company-hub publishing | MFA required; 30 min / 12 h |
| Placement mentor | Non-editing teacher (custom) | Assigned students | Mock-interview slots, rubric feedback, readiness notes | MFA required; 30 min / 12 h |
| Support | Custom role | Read-mostly, user-scoped | View account status, reset devices (quota-bound), kill sessions, handle appeals and grievances. **Cannot** set passwords (ASVS 6.4.6) ([ASVS V6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OWASP/ASVS/master/5.0/en/0x15-V6-Authentication.md)) | MFA required; step-up for session kills; 30 min / 12 h |
| Finance | Custom role | Billing data only | Plans, coupons, refunds, GST invoices, B2B purchase-order billing, reconciliation | Phishing-resistant MFA; 15 min / 8–12 h |
| Super admin | Manager at system | Platform | Role grants, tenant configuration, break-glass access, security settings | Phishing-resistant MFA (passkey); 15 min / 8–12 h; every action audited |

Several Moodle and HackerRank workflow patterns transfer directly. **Cohort sync** enrols an entire batch and keeps enrolment in step as the cohort changes. That is exactly the coordinator's need when a college uploads a revised roster ([Cohort sync](https://docs.moodle.org/en/Cohort_sync)) **[Reported, docs not re-read]**. **Per-user and per-group quiz overrides** let a trainer give extra time to a student who lost power mid-test without reopening the test for everyone ([quiz.php](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/mod/quiz/lang/en/quiz.php)) **[Verified]**. The **four-phase review matrix** lets a trainer hide answers during a placement drive and release explanations after it closes ([quiz.php](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/mod/quiz/lang/en/quiz.php)) **[Verified]**. HackerRank's practice of **locking proctoring settings once candidates start** prevents a mid-test configuration change that would invalidate comparisons ([Proctor Mode](https://hackerrank-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/5663779659-proctor-mode)) **[Verified]**. Its **evaluator-only view of hidden test cases** keeps answer keys off the client ([HackerRank KB](https://hackerrank-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/1626384486-hidden-test-cases-for-front-end%2C-back-end-and-full-stack-questions)) **[Verified]**.

The integrity-review workflow needs particular care because it turns software signals into consequences for students. HackerRank states an **overall precision of 85%** for its plagiarism flags, meaning roughly one flag in seven is wrong. Its reviewers check flags against a keystroke replay rather than acting on the flag alone ([HackerRank KB](https://hackerrank-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/7287334157-ai-plagiarism-detection)). Mettl keeps its credibility index out of shared reports ([Mettl](https://support.mettl.com/portal/en/kb/articles/candidate-credibility-index-in-proctoring-reports-18-4-2023)). An audit of a widely used proctoring suite found that faces from some racial groups were substantially more likely to produce false results ([Cohney et al.](https://am.aals.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2022/02/Cohney-Internet-and-Computer-Law.pdf)). Tech Drona's trainer console should therefore show integrity signals as "review recommended", with the evidence attached. Leaderboards stay provisional until a post-test similarity pass completes, which mirrors the Codeforces rollback practice ([Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/113185)). The penalty ladder should be published, modelled on LeetCode's one-month ban followed by permanent deactivation ([LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/discuss/post/951105/)), but with an appeal step that LeetCode explicitly omits. **[Rec]**

Finally, every privileged action should land in an append-only audit table. That covers device resets, session kills, grade overrides, refunds, coupon creation, bulk exports and role grants. OWASP lists privilege changes, sensitive data access and data import and export among the events that must always be logged ([Logging CS](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html)). DPDP Rule 6(c) independently requires logs and review of access to personal data ([Rule 6](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule6.html)). **[Verified]**

## 6. Recommended features: copy proven mechanics, publish every rule

These recommendations adopt patterns with public evidence behind them. Everything in this section is **[Rec]** unless cited.

**Content and learning.** Mirror the market taxonomy (aptitude subdivided, programming, DSA, CS core, company hubs, interview) so students find their way immediately. Store it in Moodle's polymorphic shape: one `course_module` table plus per-type tables, so completion, logging and permissions stay generic. Frame question banks as "pattern-aligned practice", never "actual questions". PrepInsta's ₹99–₹199 Infosys question-bank complaint shows how "likely questions" marketing backfires ([ConsumerComplaints](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/bycompany/prepinsta-a488452.html)).

**Assessment engine.** Store `started_at` and `deadline_at` on the server. Use autosave on change with a 5–10 s debounce, an IndexedDB mirror, idempotent answers keyed by (attempt, question, version), and server-job auto-submit at the deadline. Add a grace period and per-user overrides. This improves on Moodle's 60 s default, which Moodle itself describes as a trade-off between server load and lost work ([settings](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/mod/quiz/settings.php)). Indian campus Wi-Fi and power cuts make it essential, and a reported resume bug in another product shows the failure mode ([Tutorials Dojo](https://portal.tutorialsdojo.com/forums/discussion/bug-timed-mode-loses-many-answered-questions-on-resume/)).

**Coding judge.** In Phase 1, use Judge0 Cloud behind a backend proxy. The API key never reaches the browser, and hidden tests are sent as stdin batches from the server. Judge both CPU and wall time, because a CPU-only limit lets `sleep` tie up workers ([isolate](https://www.ucw.cz/isolate/isolate.1.html)).

**Integrity basics.** Log full-screen exits, visibility and blur changes, and paste events. Log paste events rather than blocking them, which hurts accessibility. Keep code snapshots for replay and run MOSS or JPlag similarity within each cohort. Defer webcam and screenshot AI.

**Credentials.** Issue certificates with a unique ID and an instantly live public `/verify/{id}` page, with identity confirmed before the final proctored assessment, following Coursera's identity statement ([Coursera support](https://www.coursera.support/s/article/learner-000001187?language=en_US)).

**Commerce.** Build a gateway abstraction (Razorpay, Cashfree, UPI AutoPay) behind an "enrol on payment" method, the pattern Moodle uses with `enrol_fee` ([enrol](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/tree/main/public/enrol)). Verify webhooks with HMAC over the raw body plus a timestamp tolerance (Stripe uses 5 min) and deduplicate by event ID ([Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks)). Keep subscriptions at or below ₹15,000 per debit to avoid repeated AFA. Use an Idempotency-Key header on payment and enrolment POSTs.

**Accounts and sessions.** Use opaque server-side sessions in Redis behind a Next.js backend-for-frontend (BFF), and avoid JWTs in browser storage, as both OWASP and NIST advise ([Session CS](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Session_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html); [SP 800-63B-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html)). Provide a "your devices and sessions" page, as ASVS 7.5.2 requires at Level 2 ([ASVS V7](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OWASP/ASVS/master/5.0/en/0x16-V7-Session-Management.md)). Offer passkeys and TOTP alongside SMS OTP.

**Content protection.** Use a DRM video host with server-side token generation, a dynamic name or masked-phone watermark, and FLAG_SECURE in the Android app. Enforce the concurrent-stream limit at token issuance.

**Privacy plumbing.** Give every FastAPI module a `privacy_provider` interface that declares, exports and deletes its data. Add a central request queue and a retention registry, which is Moodle's `tool_dataprivacy` pattern ([classes](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/tree/main/public/admin/tool/dataprivacy/classes)).

**Analytics shape.** Emit xAPI-shaped events (actor, verb, object, context, result) so data stays portable. Open edX uses xAPI as its default analytics standard ([Aspects ADR](https://docs.openedx.org/projects/openedx-aspects/en/latest/technical_documentation/decisions/0002_xapi.html)).

## 7. Differentiating features: six things to build in-house

These are the places where the reference platforms are weak or silent. That makes them Tech Drona's own work, not purchased components. All are **[Rec]**.

**1. A placement-readiness engine.** Build skill-tagged mastery scores across aptitude, DSA, CS core and communication. Compute percentiles against a cohort or batch, readiness per company drive based on that company's pattern, and weak-area "next practice" recommendations. The incumbents show no public evidence of this. Coursera's admin metrics focus on consumption, not readiness ([Mass.gov guide](https://www.mass.gov/doc/dcs-info-14-569a-coursera-administrator-guide/download)). Model placement entities natively (companies, drives, eligibility criteria, shortlists), which Moodle lacks entirely.

**2. A TPO / coordinator console.** Include seat-pool utilisation, batch progress, readiness distributions, mock-based shortlisting and exportable reports. FACE Prep, which claims 2,000+ college partners, runs its paid courses on a white-label LMS ([Edmingle](https://faceprep.edmingle.com/)). That suggests a purpose-built college console is a genuine selling point.

**3. Honest commerce as a feature.** Show real prices with no inflated anchors. Apply a refund window to discounted purchases too. Use identical wording on marketing pages and in the Terms. Offer one-click cancellation and pre-renewal reminders. This directly answers PrepInsta's "no refund if discounted" clause and Coursera's Terms-versus-marketing contradiction ([PrepInsta Terms](https://prepinsta.com/terms-and-conditions/); [Coursera Terms](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms)). It also anticipates the dark-pattern self-audit required from 1 Jan 2027 ([MoneyLife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/new-ecommerce-rules-to-rein-in-dark-patterns-misleading-listings-and-hidden-charges-from-1st-january/81643.html)).

**4. A transparent account-integrity centre.** Show published device, session and stream numbers, a self-service device list and reset quota, and explanations of every enforcement action that cite the signals involved. Include an in-app appeal with a stated response time and a buddy-seat upsell. No Indian peer publishes this. Netflix, Spotify and Adobe show users accept rules that come with legitimate remedies.

**5. Assessment resilience and dispute-ready integrity.** Store immutable attempt steps for regrading. Provide a keystroke code replay for reviewers, provisional leaderboards and a human-review queue. Treat disconnects as events rather than penalties. This combines the best of Moodle's attempt storage and HackerRank's review tooling without the cost and bias risk of webcam AI ([EFF](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2021/06/long-overdue-reckoning-online-proctoring-companies-may-finally-be-here)).

**6. Verified outcomes.** Publish placement-outcome claims backed by data (cohort, sample size, verification method) rather than self-reported averages such as PrepInsta's "7.4 LPA" claim ([CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/prepinsta-prime-module-acquires-100000-active-subscribers-in-90-days/)). Add a content-completeness indicator and support SLAs visible in the dashboard. Interoperability can come later as a B2B wedge: LTI 1.3 as a tool, so colleges already on Moodle or Canvas can launch Tech Drona content.

## 8. Risks, costs, technical challenges and implementation priorities

### The largest risks are legal exposure and judge security, not missing features

**Regulatory risk is front-loaded and concrete.** DPDP penalties are per-instance ceilings: **₹250 crore** for failing security safeguards, **₹200 crore** each for failing breach notification and for breaching the children's obligations, and **₹50 crore** for other breaches ([DPDPA Schedule](https://dpdpa.com/theschedule.html)) **[Verified]**. Breach notification has **no materiality threshold**. Individuals must be told "without delay", and the Board must receive a detailed report within **72 hours** ([Rule 7](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule7.html)) **[Verified]**. A second clock runs alongside it. CERT-In requires specified incidents to be reported within **6 hours**, with 180-day log retention and NTP synchronisation to NIC or NPL servers ([Ankura](https://ankura.com/insights/everything-you-need-to-know-about-indias-new-guidelines-related-to-cyber-incident-reporting-by-cert-in/)) **[Verified via commentary]**. Until DPDP s.44(2) commences, IT Act s.43A and the SPDI Rules most likely still apply ([dpdprules.org](https://dpdprules.org/blog/dpdp-18-months-to-12-months-proposal)) **[Reported]**.

**Judge security is the largest technical risk.** Judge0's 2024 escapes scored CVSS 10.0. The root causes were a `--privileged` container running as root, `--share-net` on by default, host-side symlink-following, shell interpolation and a default Postgres password ([Tanto Security](https://tantosec.com/blog/judge0/)) **[Verified]**. Judge0 also requires switching the host to **cgroup v1**, while isolate's cgroup mode requires **v2** and modern Kubernetes defaults to v2. So expect dedicated VMs rather than pods ([Judge0 CHANGELOG](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/judge0/judge0/master/CHANGELOG.md); [isolate](https://www.ucw.cz/isolate/isolate.1.html)) **[Verified]**. Judge0 is GPL-3.0. Calling it unmodified over HTTP generally does not trigger disclosure, but shipping modified on-premise appliances to colleges could. Get legal review **[Rec]**.

**Other technical challenges.** False positives from CGNAT and college NAT are the main accuracy risk ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/detecting-cgn-to-reduce-collateral-damage/)). Moodle's concurrent-login limit loses unsaved work and breaks SSO ([auth.php](https://github.com/moodle/moodle/blob/main/public/lang/en/auth.php)). Argon2id hashing is CPU and memory heavy, so run it in a threadpool and rate-limit before hashing ([OWASP Password Storage](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Password_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html)). Multi-tenant leakage is a further risk. PostgreSQL RLS is bypassed by table owners unless `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` is set, and unique, primary-key and foreign-key checks bypass RLS entirely ([PostgreSQL docs](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-rowsecurity.html)) **[Verified]**. SMS-pumping cost abuse rounds out the list (OWASP API4 and API6) ([API Top 10](https://api-security.owasp.org/editions/2023/en/0x11-t10)). passlib is unmaintained; use pwdlib or argon2-cffi ([fastapi-users #1325](https://github.com/fastapi-users/fastapi-users/issues/1325)) **[Verified]**.

### Privacy and retention: the legal floor is one year, the ceiling is yours to justify

DPDP is consent-centric. Its notice must itemise each data element and purpose, and withdrawing consent must be as easy as giving it ([Rule 3](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule3.html)). Its nine "legitimate uses" contain **no fraud-prevention or legitimate-interests ground** ([s.7](https://dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-2/section7.html)) **[Verified]**. Account-sharing detection for adults must therefore rest on itemised consent plus contract. Logging for access security is independently required by Rule 6 and is the most defensible tier ([Rule 6](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule6.html)). **[Rec]**

Edtech is **not** in the Third Schedule, which imposes a 3-year inactivity-erasure rule only on large e-commerce, gaming and social-media fiduciaries ([Third Schedule](https://dpdpa.com/schedule/schedule3.html)). Tech Drona's retention is instead governed by three things. Section 8(7) requires erasure once the purpose is served. Rules 6(e) and 8(3) set a **1-year minimum** for logs, traffic data and associated personal data. The official illustration says order and payment logs survive account deletion for that year ([Rule 8](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule8.html)). GST records must be kept for **72 months from the annual-return due date** ([CGST s.36](https://www.knowyourgst.com/gstlaw/cgst-act/36-period-of-retention-of-accounts-36/)) **[Verified]**. A proposed schedule **[Rec]**:

| Data | Proposed retention |
|---|---|
| Account profile | Life of account + 30–90 days grace |
| Authentication and login logs (IP, user agent, outcome) | **1 year (floor)**; 180 days hot in an Indian region |
| Device tokens / fingerprints | Rolling 90–180 days for scoring; not used for behavioural profiling of under-18s |
| Account-sharing score inputs / decisions and appeals | ≤180 days / 1 year after closure |
| Time-on-task and learning analytics | 12–24 months identifiable, then aggregate; never reused for ad targeting |
| Webcam or screen proctoring media (if ever adopted) | Shortest viable period, stated in the notice (HackerRank states none ([Proctor Mode](https://hackerrank-knowledge-base.help.usepylon.com/articles/5663779659-proctor-mode))) |
| Invoices and GST | 72 months from the annual-return due date |
| Parental-consent evidence | Life of child account + limitation period; store a verification token, not ID documents |
| College (B2B) student data | Per the data processing agreement with the college; return or delete at contract end |

**Minors require a separate design track.** A "child" is anyone under 18. Processing a child's data needs verifiable parental consent from an identifiable adult. Rule 10 allows verification through data the fiduciary already holds or a DigiLocker-style virtual token ([Rule 10](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule10.html)). Section 9(3) separately **prohibits tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children** ([DPDP Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)) **[Verified]**. The Fourth Schedule exempts "educational institutions" only for monitoring related to their educational activities or the safety of enrolled children. "Educational activities" is undefined, and commentators disagree on whether edtech companies qualify ([Fourth Schedule](https://dpdpa.com/schedule/schedule4.html); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/11/223-dpdp-rules-tracking-children-parental-consent/)) **[Verified / Reported]**. The conservative design has four parts **[Rec]**:

1. Gate by date of birth at sign-up.
2. Route under-18 D2C users to Rule 10 parental consent.
3. Prefer college-sponsored enrolment for minors, where Tech Drona acts as the college's processor and the college is plausibly the "educational institution".
4. Limit minors to deterministic session and device caps, with no behavioural risk scoring, no marketing pixels and no personalisation beyond learning delivery.

Time-on-task data used to deliver progress plausibly counts as an "educational activity". The same data used for upsell targeting does not.

**Timing.** The substantive DPDP duties start around **13 May 2027**; AZB gives 12 May ([AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/?p=87799)). A proposal to compress the window to 12 months was never notified ([dpdprules.org](https://dpdprules.org/blog/dpdp-18-months-to-12-months-proposal)). The E-Commerce Amendment Rules with the dark-pattern self-audit take effect **1 Jan 2027** ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/govt-tightens-ecommerce-rules-ahead-of-the-festive-season/)) **[Reported]**. Launching DPDP-compliant from day one avoids re-consenting the whole user base later. **[Rec]**

## Conclusion

The research changes where Tech Drona's hard problems lie. Content breadth and coding infrastructure are commoditised: the taxonomy is identical across incumbents, and Judge0 Cloud costs less than a mid-range laptop per month. The genuinely scarce assets are **trustworthy rules**: a refund policy that means what it says, a device policy with published numbers, integrity flags a human can explain, and readiness metrics whose method is disclosed. The incumbents' public record shows the cost of lacking them. Their enforcement lives in vague Terms, and their complaints cluster exactly where rules were opaque.

The deeper implication is that Indian law and Indian networks point the same way. CGNAT makes IP and geolocation unreliable. DPDP s.8(3) demands accurate decision inputs. Section 9(3) forbids behavioural monitoring of the 17-year-olds Tech Drona will inevitably enrol. Together they rule out the "silent fingerprint score" approach that fraud vendors sell. What remains is deterministic, server-side enforcement at the session and video-token layer, remedies before penalties, and logged reasons for every decision. That is cheaper to build and easier to defend. It is also more likely to persuade colleges and students to choose Tech Drona over incumbents whose controls they cannot see.

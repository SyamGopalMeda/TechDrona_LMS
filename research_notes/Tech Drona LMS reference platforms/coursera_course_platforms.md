# Coursera and Comparable Course Platforms: Reference for Tech Drona (Indian placement prep + LMS)

Research date: 2026-11-03. About 20 search and fetch calls. Coursera's own pages (Terms, Coursera Plus page, learner help) and Unacademy's Terms loaded and are quoted directly. Udemy's own terms and help pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so all Udemy claims rest on secondary or Udemy-community sources and are marked that way. Labels used: **[Verified-primary]** = official page fetched or official help page cited; **[Secondary]** = third-party blog, aggregator or news; **[Inference]** = my reasoning.

## 1. Course discovery and learning experience (catalogue, paths, player, quizzes, peer review, deadlines, progress)

### Takeaway
Coursera sells breadth: 10,000+ courses, with stacked credentials (course, then Specialization or Professional Certificate, then degree) as the hook. Indian placement-prep players instead sell outcome-driven cohort "batches" with live classes and mentorship. This research did not cover the detailed mechanics of the course player, quizzes and peer review, so treat that as a gap.

### Cited Findings
- Coursera Plus promises "Unlimited Access to 10,000+ Online Courses". **[Verified-primary]** — [Coursera Plus](https://www.coursera.org/courseraplus)
- Coursera's Honor Code lists "Impersonation: completing work within any Coursera activity" as a violation. This shows that graded work (quizzes and assignments) is treated as identity-bound. **[Verified-primary]** — [Coursera Terms / Honor Code](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms)
- Coursera's own explainer describes the platform and its credential tiers. **[Verified-primary, not deep-read]** — [What Is Coursera?](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-coursera)
- Udemy Personal Plan gives access to a curated subset of the catalogue (reported as "26,000+ courses"). It is not the full marketplace. **[Secondary]** — [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/udemy-personal-plan-26000-courses-build-in-demand-skills-2026)
- Indian upskilling players sell long programmes: Scaler Academy is reported at ₹3,99,000 total, with EMI "starting at ₹9,791/month" and a ₹20,000 upfront commitment. **[Secondary]** — [bootcampguide](https://www.bootcampguide.com/blog/scaler-academy-fees); [fueler.io](https://fueler.io/blog/scaler-academy-review-curriculum-placements-fees-and-roi)

### Inferences
- Tech Drona's catalogue should be organised as "paths" (for example DSA, then System Design, then Mock Interviews), each mapped to a target role or company. This mirrors Coursera Specializations and Scaler's curriculum tracks. Placement prep favours sequenced paths with deadlines over a browse-only catalogue.
- Tie graded assessments and coding tests to a verified identity, following Coursera's honor-code approach, because certificates and placement eligibility will depend on them.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch Coursera's help pages on deadlines (flexible or reset deadlines), the peer-review workflow, or the course player. I also did not research edX's course experience. These need a separate pass.
- I found no detail on the learner experience (player, test series) at Unacademy, PW or upGrad.

## 2. Enrolment and business models (audit, purchase, subscription, financial aid, coupons, refunds, Indian models)

### Takeaway
The comparison set covers five models: per-course purchase (Udemy, with heavy discounting to about ₹399 to ₹599), annual all-access subscriptions (Coursera Plus, Udemy Personal Plan, about ₹6,000 to ₹14,000 a year in India), free audit plus financial aid (Coursera), high-ticket cohort programmes with EMI (Scaler, upGrad), and auto-renewing subscriptions (Unacademy). Refund rules vary widely, from 30 days on Udemy courses to "no refund" at Unacademy.

### Cited Findings
- **Coursera Plus (India), as shown on the official page when fetched in Nov 2026:** ₹2,099/month or ₹13,999/year, with a 7-day free trial. Monthly is "cancel anytime"; annual has a "14-day money-back guarantee". **[Verified-primary]** — [Coursera Plus](https://www.coursera.org/courseraplus)
  - Conflict: secondary sources report promotional annual prices of ₹7,499 to ₹7,999 and an older "regular" price of about ₹33,000/year. Coursera introduced region-aligned India pricing ("up to 60% more affordable"). **[Secondary]** — [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/udemy-vs-coursera-price-india-2026); [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/coursera-introduces-region-aligned-pricing-to-make-learning-up-to-60-more-affordable-in-india/)
- **Coursera refunds** (Refund and Cancellation Policy inside the Terms):
  - Courses: "you may receive a full refund if you cancel within 7 days of payment".
  - Subscriptions: "Refunds for subscription plans are not provided, except (i) where required by law…", and the policy names "Coursera Lite, Coursera Plus Monthly and Annual plans".
  - Cancellation: "Cancellation is effective at the end of the current billing period."
  - **[Verified-primary]** — [Coursera Terms](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms)
  - The Terms contradict the "14-day money-back guarantee" on the Plus page; the marketing page appears to be more generous.
- **Coursera financial aid:** learners apply through the "Apply for financial aid" link on the payment screen. An approved application is one route to a certificate, alongside payment and sponsored access. **[Secondary]** — [Coursera Wiki](https://coursera.wiki/certificates/). The fetched Terms did not mention financial aid.
- **Udemy individual courses (India):** reported at ₹399 to ₹519 per course in the August 2026 sale, and ₹599 to ₹7,900 at list price. **[Secondary]** — [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/udemy-course-price-india-august-2026); [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/udemy-personal-plan-price-india-2026)
- **Udemy Personal Plan (India):** "Rs. 500.00 /month, Rs. 6,000.00 /year", billed annually (Zoutons says this was read from Udemy's India pricing panel on 21 Aug 2026). A May 2026 sale price of about ₹4,500/year was also reported. **[Secondary]** — [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/udemy-personal-plan-price-india-2026); [Medium](https://medium.com/@barnwalsaurabh/udemy-may-2026-sale-should-you-buy-individual-courses-or-the-udemy-personal-plan-37fbeac69f93)
- **Udemy lifetime access** lasts "provided that your account is in good standing and Udemy continues to have a license to that course". **[Verified-primary snippet; page returned 403 to fetch]** — [Udemy support](https://support.udemy.com/hc/articles/229603708)
- **Udemy refunds:**
  - Courses: 30 days. Udemy may refund as credit, and may deny refunds or suspend accounts for abuse.
  - Subscriptions: not covered by the 30-day guarantee.
  - iOS purchases: refunded as credit only.
  - **[Secondary]** — [Learnopoly](https://learnopoly.com/udemy-refund/); [lilachbullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/udemy-free-trial/)

On refunds: "there shall be no refund of the fee already paid", with reference to a separate Refund Policy. **[Verified-primary]** — [Unacademy Terms](https://unacademy.com/terms)
- **PW Skills** GenAI programme: "₹34,999 Premium / ₹39,999 Pro; ₹6,999 Basic (recorded)". **[Secondary]** — [logicmojo](https://logicmojo.com/affordable-scaler-academy-alternatives/)

### Inferences
- Tech Drona could combine a low-cost all-access tier (₹4,000 to ₹8,000/year, benchmarked against Udemy PP and promotional Coursera Plus) with premium cohort or placement batches (₹20k to ₹60k with EMI, below Scaler and upGrad). This captures both price-sensitive students and outcome buyers.
- Publish one consistent refund rule (for example a 7-day refund if under 20% is consumed). Coursera shows the reputational risk of marketing copy contradicting the Terms.

### Gaps
- I found no official current prices for Unacademy Plus/Iconic subscriptions or PW batches (PW batches are known to be cheap, but I found no source). The Scaler and upGrad figures are secondary.
- I did not verify official edX pricing (verified-track fees, edX subscription) or Udemy's coupon mechanics (instructor coupons, price caps).

## 3. Institutional and B2B offerings (Campus, Business, seat licences, admin dashboards, analytics)

### Takeaway
Coursera for Campus and Business and Udemy Business all sell annual per-seat licences, with an admin portal for inviting learners, tracking consumption and reporting progress. Detailed public documentation of current features was thin.

### Cited Findings
- **Coursera for Campus Basic** offers "up to 20,000 licenses for currently enrolled university students". **[Primary flyer, hosted third-party]** — [Coursera flyer (constantcontact)](https://files.constantcontact.com/32a10b83701/e5d0022e-ef5f-4a30-9626-af765730c8a9.pdf)
- **Coursera admin portal** (older guides), per a state-government guide and the 2020 Coursera Admin Guide:
  - The Summary tab shows high-level metrics.
  - Data refreshes daily, and license-consumption metrics update every 24 hours.
  - The Manage Learners sub-tab lists enrolled learners.
  - The portal link comes from the Coursera customer team.
  - **[Primary but dated]** — [Mass.gov Coursera Administrator Guide](https://www.mass.gov/doc/dcs-info-14-569a-coursera-administrator-guide/download); [Coursera Admin Guide 2020](https://www.rshu.edu.ua/images/indsam_rob_stud/Coursera_Admin_Guide_2020.pdf)
- **Indian university adoption:** for example, Arka Jain University held a Coursera refresher session in 2025. **[Primary, institutional]** — [Arka Jain report](https://arkajainuniversity.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Event-Report_Coursera-Refresher-Session.pdf)
- **Udemy Business Team plan:** an annual subscription priced per person and paid upfront, where price = users × per-user rate + taxes, varying by location. **[Primary snippet]** — [Udemy Business support](https://business-support.udemy.com/hc/articles/18981435834775/)
  - Reported list price: USD 360/user/year ($30/mo billed annually), for 2 to 20 or 2 to 50 seats (sources conflict). Enterprise pricing is unpublished. **[Secondary]** — [marcandrews](https://marcandrews.com/udemy-business-review-2026-worth-it-for-uk-teams/); [trainingcost](https://trainingcost.com/udemy-business-pricing)
- **edX for Business** documents licence assignment from an admin dashboard. **[Verified-primary]** — [edX Business support: How do I assign a license](https://business-support.edx.org/hc/en-us/articles/1500008018061-How-do-I-assign-a-license); [edX reporting](https://business-support.edx.org/hc/en-us/categories/1500000804242-Reporting)

### Inferences
- Tech Drona's B2B (college) product needs four things:
  - A seat pool with assign and revoke, and reassignment when students graduate.
  - Daily-refreshed dashboards: licence utilisation, per-student progress and test scores, and placement-readiness scores.
  - CSV or roster import.
  - Exportable reports for TPOs (training and placement officers).
- Per-seat annual pricing paid upfront is the industry norm. Indian colleges may also need a GST invoice and PO-based billing.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch current Coursera Campus/Business admin help or Udemy Business admin and insights documentation. Licence reassignment rules are unverified for Coursera and Udemy.

## 4. Progress tracking and certificates (verification, shareable URLs, ID verification)

### Takeaway
Coursera issues certificates with a public verification link and states that it confirmed the learner's identity. This is the benchmark for Tech Drona's placement certificates.

### Cited Findings
- Learners open their Certificates tab, click "Share Certificate" and copy a publicly accessible link "that can be accessed by anyone". **[Verified-primary]** — [Coursera support: Share your certificates](https://www.coursera.support/s/article/learner-000001329?language=en_US)
- Course Certificates carry a statement that Coursera confirmed the identity of the learner. **[Verified-primary]** — [About Course Certificates](https://www.coursera.support/s/article/learner-000001187?language=en_US)
- A new verification URL can reportedly take 24 to 48 hours to activate, and users have reported broken verification links. **[Secondary / community]** — [CertFusion](https://certfusion.com/r/coursera-shareable-certificates-explained-everything-you-need-to-know); [Coursera community](https://www.coursera.support/s/question/0D51U00003BlWAiSAN/verification-of-certificates?language=en_US)
- Historical context: Coursera's 2013 "Signature Track" used ID and typing-pattern verification. **[Primary, historical]** — [Coursera blog](https://blog.coursera.org/signaturetrack); [ACM Ubiquity](https://ubiquity.acm.org/article.cfm?id=2591684)

### Inferences
- Tech Drona should issue certificates with a unique ID and a public verification page (`/verify/{id}`) that works immediately. A LinkedIn "Add to profile" button and identity checks before proctored final assessments would make certificates credible to recruiters.

### Gaps
- I did not check Udemy's certificate verification (Udemy certificates have a URL but no ID verification; not verified here). I also did not check edX verified-certificate ID checks or current Coursera ID-verification mechanics (photo ID or webcam).

## 5. Account sharing and device policies

### Takeaway
Every platform checked forbids account sharing in its Terms. No platform publishes an exact device count or concurrent-stream limit in the documents I could access. Enforcement detail (forced logout, device caps) is found only in anecdotal or secondary sources.

### Cited Findings
- **Coursera Terms ("Our License to You"):** you agree to "create, access, and/or use only one user account, unless expressly permitted by Coursera" and "you will not share access to your account or access information for your account with any third party." **[Verified-primary]** — [Coursera Terms](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms)
- **Coursera Acceptable Use Policy** prohibits: "Share your password, let anyone besides yourself access your account". **[Verified-primary]** — [Coursera Terms/AUP](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms)
- **Udemy:**
  - The licence is reportedly "limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable … for personal, non-commercial, educational purposes" (third-party summary of the Udemy Terms). **[Secondary]** — [ConductAtlas](https://conductatlas.com/platform/udemy/udemy-terms-of-use/non-transferable-course-access-license-and-content-removal/)
  - A Udemy community moderator stated that accounts "cannot be shared between users, as this is considered a violation of our Terms". **[Semi-official]** — [Udemy Community](https://community.udemy.com/en/discussion/159421/how-do-we-set-it-up-so-that-two-people-working-on-different-sites-can-access-one-account)
  - The official udemy.com/terms and the mobile-download help page returned 403, so I could not quote the exact clause or confirm device/download limits.
- **Unacademy Terms:**
  - Section C: "your account is personal to you and agree not to provide any other person with access to the Platform".
  - Section D(5): "The Subscription is of a personal nature and is solely for the benefit of the person subscribing" and "is not allowed to be resold by you or transferred to or shared with any other person for consideration or otherwise".
  - The Terms contain **no** clause on the number of devices or simultaneous login.
  - **[Verified-primary]** — [Unacademy Terms](https://unacademy.com/terms)
- **Physics Wallah:** a third-party blog says the app can be used on multiple devices "but you cannot use it simultaneously", that logging in on a second device may log out the first, and that the website and app can apparently run on two devices at once. I found no official PW policy. **[Secondary, low authority]** — [Primebook blog](https://www.primebook.in/blog/how-to-use-physics-wallah-app-on-two-devices)

### Inferences
- The industry pattern is a contractual ban in the Terms plus technical soft enforcement through single active session on apps and forced logout. Tech Drona can legitimately enforce:
  - Explicit Terms language, mirroring Coursera's "one account" and "no sharing access information" clauses.
  - A cap on registered devices (for example 2) with a limited number of device swaps per month.
  - One concurrent video stream per account, with a "logged in on another device" prompt.
  - Watermarking with user ID or phone number on video to deter screen recording.
- These measures are an inference of best practice; no platform's public documentation confirmed the numbers.

### Gaps
- Exact Udemy device or download limits could not be verified (support pages blocked).
- Unacademy and PW device counts or concurrent-stream limits were not found in any official source.
- I found no public evidence of concurrent-streaming limits for Coursera.

## 6. Payment gateways in India, GST invoicing, UPI AutoPay and RBI e-mandate rules

### Takeaway
Recurring billing in India must follow RBI's e-mandate framework, consolidated as the "Digital Payments – E-Mandate Framework, 2026" in April 2026. Its core rules:
- Additional factor authentication (AFA) at mandate registration.
- Silent debits only up to ₹15,000 per transaction; education is not among the ₹1 lakh exempt categories.
- A pre-debit notification at least 24 hours before each debit.
- Users can pause, modify or cancel at any time.

Razorpay Subscriptions supports cards, UPI AutoPay and e-mandate under these rules.

### Cited Findings
- **RBI 2022:** the AFA limit for e-mandates was increased from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 per transaction (circular of 16 June 2022). **[Primary/legal]** — [RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12341); [Khaitan & Co](https://www.khaitanco.com/thought-leaderships/RBI-enhances-transaction-limits-for-processing-of-e-mandates-for-recurring-transactions:-from-INR-5000-to-INR-15000)
- **RBI December 2023:** the ₹1 lakh no-AFA limit was raised for specific categories (mutual funds, insurance, credit card bills). **[Secondary]** — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/interviews/rbi-raises-limit-of-e-mandates-for-recurring-online-transactions-to-1-lakh-123120801110_1.html); [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12570)
- **E-Mandate Framework 2026** (April 2026), per Business Today:
  - Registration and the first transaction need AFA (they can be combined).
  - Later debits of up to ₹15,000 go through without AFA; above ₹15,000 needs AFA.
  - ₹1 lakh no-AFA applies to insurance, mutual funds and credit card bills.
  - The pre-debit notification must reach the customer at least 24 hours ahead, with merchant name, amount and date.
  - Users can pause, modify or cancel at any time.
  - Variable-amount mandates allow a user-set maximum.
  - Every mandate must state its validity period, and a post-debit alert is required.
  - **[Secondary news; primary 2026 circular not fetched]** — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/now-control-your-auto-debit-payments-rbis-new-rules-explained-526860-2026-04-22); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-rbi-additional-factor-authentication-e-mandates/); [IANS](https://ianslive.in/rbi-tightens-e-mandate-rules-makes-extra-authentication-mandatory-for-recurring-payments-above-rs-15000--20260421192243)
- **UPI limit for education:** RBI raised the UPI per-transaction limit for payments to hospitals and educational institutions to ₹5 lakh in December 2023. This is a UPI payment cap, not an e-mandate exemption. **[Secondary]** — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/tax-cases/rbi-raises-upi-transaction-limit-hospitals-educational-institutions-244062?infinitescroll=1)
- **Razorpay Subscriptions** payment methods:
  - Cards: "Card details are securely tokenised as per RBI guidelines".
  - UPI AutoPay: "via all major UPI apps including PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM and Amazon Pay".
  - Emandate: "via Aadhaar, netbanking or debit card-based mandates".
  - **[Verified-primary]** — [Razorpay Subscriptions docs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/subscriptions/)
- **Razorpay UPI AutoPay mechanics:**
  - The pre-debit notification is sent at least 24 hours before the debit; Razorpay's FAQ cites a 25-hour gap for subsequent charges.
  - Debits above ₹15,000 need extra customer authorisation.
  - NPCI caps on the maximum mandate amount depend on the merchant's MCC, and variable ("as_presented") mandates have lower ceilings.
  - Debit webhooks arrive 24 to 36 hours after the charge is triggered.
  - **[Verified-primary]** — [Razorpay UPI AutoPay](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/recurring-payments/upi/); [Razorpay recurring integration](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/recurring-payments/overview/integrate); [Razorpay UPI FAQs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/international-payments/accept-international-payments-from-indian-customers/s2s-integration/recurring-payments/upi/faqs/)
- **Stripe** documents UPI AutoPay support for India. **[Verified-primary, not deep-read]** — [Stripe UPI AutoPay](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/upi/upi-autopay)
- **Juspay** offers UPI AutoPay and mandate orchestration. **[Verified-primary, not deep-read]** — [Juspay](https://juspay.io/in/docs/upi-autopay)
- Unacademy's Terms say it uses "third-party payment gateways and/or aggregators" without naming them. **[Verified-primary]** — [Unacademy Terms](https://unacademy.com/terms)

### Inferences
- **Billing design for Tech Drona:**
  - Subscriptions of ₹15,000 or less per debit avoid repeat OTP friction. A ₹4k to ₹8k annual plan or a ₹299 to ₹999 monthly plan fits.
  - Charge larger batch fees (₹20k+) as a one-time payment, an EMI/cardless-EMI product, or split instalments, rather than as a silent mandate.
  - Build dunning logic around the 24-hour pre-debit window and the delayed webhooks.
- **GST:** online education services sold by private (non-exempt) providers are generally taxed at 18% under SAC 9992 / OIDAR rules. Tech Drona would need GSTIN-based tax invoices and B2B invoices carrying the buyer's GSTIN. Razorpay, Cashfree and PayU offer invoicing modules. This is not verified in this research; confirm with a CA.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the primary text of the RBI 2026 e-mandate framework, so details come from news reports.
- I found no sources on Cashfree or PayU subscription docs, GST invoicing features of gateways, or which gateways Coursera, Udemy, PW or Unacademy actually use in India.

## 7. Lessons and opportunities for Tech Drona

### Takeaway
Tech Drona can combine Coursera's credential credibility (verifiable, identity-bound certificates and institutional dashboards) with Udemy and Coursera India's affordable all-access pricing and Indian edtech's outcome-driven batches. It can differentiate through strict but fair anti-sharing enforcement, RBI-compliant UPI AutoPay billing, and college (TPO) dashboards.

### Cited Findings
- Coursera regionalised Indian pricing ("up to 60% more affordable"), which signals Indian price sensitivity. — [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/coursera-introduces-region-aligned-pricing-to-make-learning-up-to-60-more-affordable-in-india/)
- Annual all-access benchmarks: Udemy PP about ₹6,000/yr; Coursera Plus ₹13,999/yr list with frequent promotions down to about ₹7,499. — [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/udemy-personal-plan-price-india-2026); [Coursera Plus](https://www.coursera.org/courseraplus)
- High-ticket placement programmes (Scaler ₹3.99 lakh) use EMI to reduce upfront cost. — [bootcampguide](https://www.bootcampguide.com/blog/scaler-academy-fees)
- Every reviewed platform makes accounts personal and non-transferable in its Terms. — [Coursera Terms](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms); [Unacademy Terms](https://unacademy.com/terms)

### Inferences
- **Pricing ladder:** a free tier (sample lessons and a practice test), an all-access subscription (UPI AutoPay, ≤ ₹15,000 per debit), premium placement batches (one-time or EMI), and college seat licences (annual, GST invoice).
- **Trust:** a single, plain-language refund policy consistent across the marketing pages and the Terms; public certificate verification; and an anti-sharing clause plus device limit communicated up front, with UI such as "You're logged in on another device – continue here?".
- **B2B differentiation:** a TPO dashboard with placement-readiness analytics (test scores, coding stats, mock-interview ratings), which Coursera's consumption-focused admin metrics do not emphasise.

### Gaps
- Competitor B2B pricing in India (Coursera for Campus INR pricing, Unacademy/PW institutional offers) was not found.

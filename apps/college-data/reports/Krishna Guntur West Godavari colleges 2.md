# Win tens per campus, sign colleges for hundreds

*Krishna, Guntur and West Godavari B.Tech colleges: research report v2 for Tech Drona, 7 November 2026. Replaces the earlier report of the same name for planning.*

## Answer in brief

**89 B.Tech colleges are actively running in the old (undivided) Krishna, Guntur and West Godavari districts, and Tech Drona should target 84 of them in 2026.** The other 5 active colleges are on Hold (no usable data or no final-year IT batch). A further 41 rows in the portal list are not targets: 21 look closed, 6 are winding down with no new intake, 4 run pharmacy only, 1 is listed but admitted nobody, 1 is unverified, 5 are duplicates and 3 are outside these districts.

**The final-year IT-related pool (CSE, IT, AI-ML, data science and allied branches) is about 40,162 students, and 51% of it (20,470) is verified on an official document.** The rest is estimated by a stated method. Counting all branches, the final-year pool is about 60,764, 76% verified.

**Planned paid enrollments by 31 December 2026 are 377 (minimum), 655 (average) and 1,143 (maximum) across the 84 targeted colleges.** The minimum is 94% of the company-wide goal of 400, so these districts alone fall 23 short in the worst case; with the outer Godavari colleges from the company blueprint (about 83 more at minimum), the combined minimum is about 460.

**No college can reach 400 enrollments on its own through students paying individually.** The largest single-college average is KL University's 49. 31 colleges (AMBER) could pass 400 only through an institution-paid agreement (MoU) covering 2nd- and 3rd-year IT students, starting January 2027. 58 colleges (RED) cannot reach 400 by any route modelled. None is GREEN.

**Start with the 24 P1 colleges**, led by Narasaraopeta Engineering College (NEC), KL University, VFSTR, SRKR, SRGEC, RVR & JC, Swarnandhra and VIT-AP. Together they hold 22,414 IT final-years and 203 of the 377 minimum.

**The team to build is 1 Programme Manager, 1 Payments & Data Verifier, 12 District/City Leads, 64 College Leads, 299 Campus Ambassadors and 95 standby ambassadors.** Nobody is hired yet; the tracker shows 0 people and 0 enrollments. College Leads are not named in this report on purpose: they are chosen through TPO/HOD nomination, a scored interview and a 7-day trial (Section 4).

**Lead with a different offer by college type.** At the universities, lead with a "build and ship" GenAI/full-stack project bootcamp. At big autonomous colleges, lead with a "Ninja → Digital" upgrade that prepares students for the coding tests that decide the roughly 2x-paid premium tracks at TCS, Infosys and Cognizant. At smaller colleges, lead with a low-priced aptitude-plus-coding entry module. Price well below the ₹20,000–60,000 bootcamps and never compete with free state certificates.

**The reported APSCHE 360-hour internship mandate is not officially confirmed.** APSCHE says all internships must be free to students and has publicly warned firms that charged fees. Tech Drona cannot sell a course as "the internship"; at most it can register as a free host.

**Track everything in the v2 tracker workbook.** Open and save it once in Excel so its formulas calculate. Corrections to the portal database have been found but are not applied, pending approval.

### Terms used once and throughout

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified (V)** | Read from an official document: AICTE approval (EoA), NIRF filing, mandatory disclosure, annual report, NBA filing or the state counselling list. |
| **Estimated (E)** | Worked out by a stated method, for example 2026 seats × 2026 fill rate, or a verified total × the region's median IT share (0.60). Approximate. |
| **Unavailable (n/a)** | Not found. Never guessed; ask on the first call. |
| **Final-year pool** | Students who entered in 2023-24 and are now in 4th year. "IT pool" means the IT-related branches only. |
| **Min / Avg / Max** | Planning range of paid enrollments by 31 Dec 2026: cautious, expected, stretch. These are built from assumed conversion rates (0.75% / 1.25% / 2.0% of IT final-years, plus 2nd- and 3rd-years at half weight). They are not forecasts. |
| **P1 / P2 / P3 / Hold** | Priority from a 100-point score: P1 (65+) start now, P2 (45–64.9) next, P3 (below 45) later, Hold = no 2026 target. |
| **GREEN / AMBER / RED** | The 400-per-college test. GREEN: average ≥ 400 from individual sign-ups. AMBER: 400 reachable only with an institution-paid MoU from Jan 2027 (large university or autonomous college). RED: not reachable. |
| **Lead offer (a)–(d)** | (a) university project bootcamp; (b) "Ninja → Digital" placement upgrade; (c) entry aptitude + coding module; (d) women's college, run as (b) or (c) by size. |

## 2. Student strength: 40,162 IT final-years, half verified

### How strength was measured

The final-year pool uses the best available source, in this order. First, **actual 2023-24 admissions** from a primary document; these exist for VIT-AP, SMCE, Usha Rama, SRGEC, Sir CRR and MAM Women's. Second, **2023-24 sanctioned seats** from the AICTE approval or NIRF, with AICTE winning any conflict. Third, a **stated estimate**. The portal database's own figure is 2026-27 intake, which overstates fast-growing colleges, so it is used only as a last resort.

### The biggest corrections this round

**VIT-AP is far larger than estimated.** Its 2023-24 annual report shows 3,492 B.Tech students admitted in 2023, of whom 3,025 are in CSE branches (CSE 2,798 + CSBS 227), against the earlier estimate of 1,581 ([VIT-AP Annual Report 2023-24](https://universitywebsitbucket.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/Annual+Reports/Annual+Report_2023-24.pdf)). That makes it the largest verified IT pool in the region. The 572 integrated M.Tech students are excluded. **NEC's 1,500 seats (IT 960) are confirmed.** Its AICTE approval for 2023-24 gives 1,500 ([NEC AICTE EoA 2023-24](https://www.nrtec.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/AICTE-2023-24-1.pdf)), and an NBA filing confirms it at branch level ([NEC NBA CSE-AI 2026](https://www.nrtec.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NBA-CSE-AI-DCS-2026.pdf)). NIRF's 960 for every year is formulaic and is rejected. **VVIT** leaves Hold with 1,740 sanctioned seats in 2023-24 ([VVIT NIRF 2025](https://www.vvitguntur.com/images/documents/NIRF/NIRF-VVIT-2025.pdf)). **RGUKT Nuzvid** leaves Hold with an estimated batch of about 1,100, based on 1,000 + 100 EWS seats per campus ([Deccan Chronicle](https://deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/4400-students-selected-for-rgukt-in-ap-1809741)). **Vishnu Institute (VITB)** is now 1,140 (IT 780), taken from its 2023-24 AICTE approval ([VITB EoA 2023-24](https://vishnu.edu.in/Docs/AICTE/EOA_Report_202324_VIT.pdf)).

Some colleges fill far fewer seats than they are sanctioned. **Sri Mittapalli (SMCE)** was sanctioned 540 but admitted 393 in 2023-24 (IT 363), and filled only 13% of its 2026 counselling seats ([SMCE mandatory disclosure](https://www.smce.ac.in/pdfs/mandatory-disclosure.pdf)). **Mandava IET and Nimra** report only 436 and 408 UG students across all four years ([MIET NIRF 2025](https://mandava.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/MANDAVA-INSTITUTE-OF-ENGINEERING-AND-TECHNOLOGY20250109-Engineering.pdf); [Nimra NIRF 2026](https://nimracet.in/images/downloads/nirf/nirf-2026.pdf)). Their batches are estimated at about 110 and 115. **Velaga Nageswara Rao CE (VNRCE)** filled 2 of 360 seats in 2026, so its IT final-year pool is estimated at about 1 student. Usha Rama, by contrast, admitted 542 against 540 sanctioned ([Usha Rama mandatory disclosure](https://cdn.usharama.edu.in/documents/mandatory-disclosure.pdf)). SRGEC admitted 1,318 ([SRGEC mandatory disclosure 2026](https://gecgudlavalleru.ac.in/images/admin/pdf/1707315874_Mandatory%20Disclosure%202026%20final.pdf)) and Sir CRR 1,039 ([CRR mandatory disclosure](https://sircrrengg.ac.in/images/MANDATORYDISCLOSURE/MANDATORY_DISCLOSURE_2025-26.pdf)).

### Identify: nomination, not data harvesting

No College Lead is named here, and no student's personal data was collected, by design. Candidates come in through institutions. The TPO or placement cell nominates two pre-final or final-year students who already coordinate drives. An HOD or club faculty adviser nominates office-bearers of ACM, CSI, IEEE or GDG. Fest and hackathon coordinator roles are approached only through the faculty coordinator or the club's official handle. A public application form adds inbound candidates. Tech Drona stores a person's details only after that person submits the form. Google's student-club lead selection (application, short video, interview, at least a year left before graduation) is a good template ([Google GDSC leads](https://developers.google.cn/community/gdsc/leads?hl=en)).

## 5. College-wise targets and the 400-per-college answer

### How a target is set

Each college's target comes from one formula: **target = type factor × contact factor × (rate × IT final-years + half-weight rate × IT 3rd-years + half-weight rate × IT 2nd-years)**. The rates are 0.75% / 1.25% / 2.0% for min / avg / max. The type factor raises or lowers the rate by college type. The contact factor is 1.0 with a verified TPO line, 0.8 with an office or principal line, and 0.6 with none. Any college with final-years and a usable contact gets at least 2. The rates are planning assumptions, anchored on an industry lead-to-enrollment index of about 1% ([The Wire/PTI, Meritto index](https://m.thewire.in/article/ptiprnews/ai-led-discovery-converts-6x-better-than-digital-reveals-merittos-enrollment-index-2026)). Replace them with Tech Drona's own measured rates after two weeks of selling.

### Is 400 enrollments per college feasible?

**Not from students paying individually, anywhere.** At the planning rates of up to 2% of eligible final-years, 400 paid enrollments would need 20,000 or more final-years in one college, and the largest pool here is VIT-AP's 3,025. The largest voluntary averages are KL's 49 and VIT-AP's 36. Public benchmarks agree. TalentSprint's paid, selective Pega programme reached "around 2,000 students per year across 20 partner colleges", or about 100 per college ([CRN](https://www.crn.in/?p=58674)). Whole-cohort numbers appear only when the institution or the state pays. The APSSDC × Indo-European programme ran at a quota of 250 students per college ([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/posts/index/Andhra-Pradesh/2018-11-04/APSSDC-inks-MoU-with-German-firm-to-train-students/436875)).

**How this fits the company-wide 400.** These districts' minimum of 377 is 94% of 400; the average of 655 is 164% and the maximum of 1,143 is 286%. The company blueprint's committed plan put about 83 of its minimum in Godavari colleges outside this scope. Keep those, and the combined minimum is about 460.

### The 360-hour internship: say it plainly

**This is not confirmed, and even if it is, Tech Drona cannot charge for it.** The only sources are a GKToday article of 4 November 2026 and one site repeating it. It reports a 360-hour, 12-credit, final-year internship from 16 November 2026, with industry partners registering on APSCHE's Institute-Industry Connect portal ([GKToday](https://www.gktoday.in/andhra-pradesh-mandates-360-hour-internships-for-undergraduates/)). No APSCHE circular or GO was found on its homepage or circulars page ([APSCHE](https://apsche.ap.gov.in/)). APSCHE's own alert names firms that asked students for ₹3,500 and states that "all the internships provided are at free of cost and students need not pay anything to any company or individual" ([APSCHE internship alert](https://apsche.ap.gov.in/internalert.php)).

So Tech Drona must not sell a course as "the internship". At most it can register on the IIC portal as a free host, preferably stipended, and keep paid courses separate and voluntary. For B.Tech students the relevant semester is 4-2 (January–April 2027), not November–December. Ask two or three TPOs whether they have received the circular.

## Gaps and limitations

1. **The minimum is still 23 short of 400 in these districts alone**, and 49% of the IT pool is estimated. That includes KL's 2,940, the largest pool. SRM AP has no data at all.
2. **NEC's 2026 admissions status needs one phone call.** This round's search of the counselling list did not find NEC under "NARAS", but the earlier evidence recorded it there under code NSPE (1,632 seats). The miss is most likely a naming mismatch; confirm it on the first TPO call.
3. **Closures are inferred, not proven.** The AICTE closure dashboard was unreachable, and no JNTUK affiliation list for 2025-26 is online. Phone-check winding-down colleges, NVR CET especially, before any contact.
4. **Contacts.** Six colleges lack a TPO or HOD line. VITB's listed number is an office line on its TPO page, but it is scored as a TPO line. Principal emails from the state list are on gmail, yahoo or rediffmail addresses.
5. **Floor targets.** VNRCE (IT pool about 1), Mandava, Nimra, WISE, Priyadarshini Women's and Priyadarshini ITM sit at the minimum floor of 2 / 2 / 2 with 3 ambassadors each. Phone-check their batch sizes before staffing them.
6. **Exam dates.** 4-1 end dates are unknown for 31 of the 89 active colleges, including KL, VFSTR and VIT-AP. JNTUK lab-exam dates are unannounced.
7. **The MoU pipeline is understated** at VIT-AP, where current intake was used instead of about 3,000+ admitted a year, and at VVIT and RGUKT, where 2nd- and 3rd-years are counted as 0.
8. **Demand is inferred, not surveyed.** It comes from two college placement sheets and national surveys, not from local students. Social follower counts were not read, because they need a login.
9. **Every conversion rate, type factor, MoU coverage figure and incentive amount is an assumption.** Replace them after two weeks of measured data.

# Few delta colleges were sitting 3-1 or 4-1 exams on 3 November 2026

On the reference date, 3 November 2026, only **6 of 128 colleges had a 3-1 (Sem V) exam window open and 5 had a 4-1 (Sem VII) window open**. That is 11 college-semester rows out of 256, and every one of them is an autonomous college or university running its own exams. The single largest group, **66 colleges whose 4-1 batch and 51 whose 3-1 batch sit the JNTUK timetable**, had nothing running: JNTUK's revised CoE notice of 29-10-2026 moved R23 Mid-II from 02-07 Nov to **09-13 Nov (3-1) and 09-14 Nov (4-1)**, set end-semester theory for **17, 19, 21, 24, 26 Nov (3-1) and 16, 18, 20, 23, 25, 27 Nov (4-1)**, and postponed lab externals until after the theory exams with no dates yet. Overall, 3-1 splits into 65 Upcoming, 6 In Progress, 31 No Current Exams Found and 26 Unverified; 4-1 into 79 Upcoming, 5 In Progress, 32 No Current Exams Found and 12 Unverified. No row is Completed: wherever a mid exam had finished, a later end-semester window was still due. Every In Progress row rests on a published timetable or college notice. Ten are timetables; RVR&JC is a notice about an internal series that was paused on 3 Nov. A further 16 rows whose 2026-27 calendar window covers 3 Nov, with no timetable behind it, are classed Unverified under one uniform rule. And 3 Nov itself was a Cyclone Murasu holiday in at least Guntur and Bapatla districts, so 'In Progress' means the exam window was open, not that a paper was written that day. The companion file `BTech exam status 3-1 4-1 Nov 2026.csv` holds all 256 rows (128 colleges × 2 semesters) with exam body, separate end-sem, mid and lab windows, evidence label, confidence, source URL and notes.

Every count in this report was computed by script from the CSV, not by hand. Remaining days = calendar days from 2026-11-03 to the end date of the in-progress exam window, counting the end date and not 3 Nov (end date minus 3 Nov).

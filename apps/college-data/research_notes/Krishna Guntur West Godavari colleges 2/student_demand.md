# Student interest and course demand: B.Tech students in coastal AP (Krishna, NTR/Vijayawada, Guntur, West Godavari), 2025–2026

Research date: 7 Nov 2026. Builds on (does not repeat) `playbook_leads_ambassadors.md`, `marketing_conversion_plan.md`, `social_communities.md` and `BTech colleges placements 2024 to 2026.md`. Facts already there (Unstop 83%/85% unplaced and 92% "live projects", ₹3,000–5,000 short-course prices, NPTEL ₹1,000 exam fee, APSCHE–SmartInternz free virtual internships, APSCHE 360-h internship from 16 Nov 2026, FACE Prep CRT ₹50,000–70,000, KL embedded certifications, GDG/IEEE chapter lists, fest calendar) are cited there and only referred to as [PRIOR].

Labels: **[SOURCED]** carries a URL; **[PRIOR]** = in the earlier notes with source; **[INFERRED]** = my reasoning from sourced facts; **[ARITHMETIC]** = computed from a cited table. No statistics invented.

Single most useful new finding: **SRKR's own 2025-26 placement sheet** (extracted from the college PDF) shows that, within the mass recruiters, the gap between the "ordinary" and "premium" tracks of the same company (TCS Ninja vs Digital/Prime, Infosys SE vs DSE/SP, Cognizant GenC vs GenC Next) is roughly **2x salary**, and the premium track is decided by a coding/DSA test. That is the clearest, evidence-based thing a student in this region would pay to improve.

## Q1. Which skills/courses are in demand, and what do campus recruiters test for?

### Takeaway
Recruiter volume at the region's colleges is dominated by TCS, Cognizant and Infosys mass hiring at 3.4–4.5 LPA; their selection tests are aptitude + reasoning + verbal + coding. The paid-worthy demand is the **coding/DSA step-up to the premium tracks** (TCS Digital/Prime 7–9 LPA, Infosys DSE/SP 7–11 LPA, Cognizant GenC Next 6.75 LPA), with AI-tool literacy as an emerging add-on. Generic "certificate" courses in AI/cloud are crowded by free state programmes (see Q3).

### Cited Findings
**What recruiters at these colleges actually hired (college-published 2025-26 tables)**
- **SRKR Engineering College, Bhimavaram (West Godavari), batch 2025-26: 2,323 offers, 1,339 students placed** (branch-wise placed: CSE 217, ECE 228, MECH 174, EEE 130, CIVIL 125, AIML 121, AIDS 119, IT 114, CSG 45, CIC 39, CSBS 27) — [SRKR 2025-26 placement PDF](https://www.srkrec.ac.in/assets/documents/placements/2025-26.pdf) (text extracted with pdftotext; company-to-row mapping is by row order because the PDF's layout splits names from rows — the TCS Digital/Ninja and Cognizant GenC Next/Pro rows match a search-engine extract of the same PDF, which validates the mapping).
  - TCS: Ninja (Assistant System Engineer) **232 offers @ 3.36 LPA**; Digital (System Engineer) **64 @ 7 LPA**; Prime (Software Developer) **5 @ 9 LPA**.
  - Cognizant: GenC (Programmer Analyst Trainee) **304 @ 4.1 LPA**; GenC Next (Programmer Analyst) **26 @ 6.75 LPA**; GenC Pro **3 @ 5.4 LPA**.
  - Infosys: SE (System Engineer) **79 @ 3.6 LPA**; DSE (Digital Specialist Engineer) **42 @ 7 LPA**; SP (Specialist Programmer) **11 @ 11 LPA**; Infosys L2 (Specialist Programmer L2) **1 @ 16 LPA**.
  - Accenture ASE: **2 offers @ 4.5 LPA** (row-order mapping, lower confidence). No Wipro, HCL or Capgemini rows in 2025-26.
  - Non-IT/"anyone" roles absorb many ECE/EEE/MECH/CIVIL students at 2–3.6 LPA (e.g. graduate trainee rows of 221, 110, 102, 98, 87, 81 offers), plus a customer-service row (28 @ 3.6 LPA) and a sales row (46 @ 5 LPA).

- [PRIOR] Batch-2025 medians cluster at 3.0–4.6 LPA for the autonomous JNTUK colleges; Tier 1 universities 6.4–8.7 LPA — `BTech colleges placements 2024 to 2026.md`.

**What the tests test**
- TCS NQT 2026: Foundation (aptitude/numerical, reasoning, verbal) + Advanced (advanced quant/reasoning + coding) in one sitting on TCS iON, ~190 min; the Advanced section decides Digital/Prime eligibility; no negative marking (prep-site descriptions, not TCS's own page) — [FACE Prep](https://www.faceprep.in/article/tcs-exam-pattern/); [PlacementPreparation](https://www.placementpreparation.io/tcs-nqt/syllabus-and-test-pattern/); [PrepInsta](https://prepinsta.com/tcs-nqt/syllabus/).
- Cognizant GenC 2026: online aptitude test → "Automata Fix" coding round → technical interview → HR; GenC Elevate/Next is the higher tier — [FACE Prep GenC Elevate](https://faceprep.in/article/cognizant-genc-elevate-2026-ai-readiness-for-freshers/); [NewLeaf GenC overview](https://www.newleafins.org/placement-prep/cognizant-genc/overview).
- Infosys HackWithInfy (route to SP/DSE): ~3 coding problems of rising difficulty in ~3 hours (arrays/strings, searching/sorting, graphs BFS/DFS; hard tier combinatorics/backtracking); SP is "DSA-focused coding test + technical interview" — [PlacementPreparation HackWithInfy](https://www.placementpreparation.io/hackwithinfy/syllabus-and-test-pattern/); [jobrise guide](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/infosys-interview-questions-freshers/). Sources disagree on the problems-solved cutoff for SP vs DSE.

**Hiring volume and skill direction, 2026**
- Cognizant targets 24,000–25,000 freshers in 2026 (~20% above ~20,000 in 2025), framed as AI-enabled "broader pyramid" — [Hans India](https://assets.thehansindia.com/tech/cognizant-bets-on-ai-ready-talent-plans-to-hire-up-to-25000-fresh-graduates-in-2026-1045995); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/cognizant-plans-to-hire-25000-freshers-in-2026-cfo-jatin-dalal-confirms).
- TCS ~25,000 campus offers so far, "on track" for ~40,000 freshers/year; Infosys ~20,000 graduates in FY27; demand areas named by a VC: software development, data/analytics, cloud, cybersecurity, AI-enabled roles; students expected to pair core skills with AI-tool use — [Sightsinplus citing BusinessLine](https://www.sightsinplus.com/news/employment/campus-hiring-picks-up-in-2026-as-fresher-demand-gains-momentum/).
- Unstop Talent Report 2026 (37,000+ students, 500+ HR): top in-demand skills problem-solving (49%) and AI/ML (39%); 64% of HR define top talent by skills (AI/ML, data, cloud, cybersecurity); **46% of engineering students have no formal AI training** — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/deeptech/nearly-50-hr-leaders-bet-on-ai-problem-solving-skills-to-dominate-2026-jobs); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/jobs-are-there-but-most-students-arent-getting-them-heres-why).

### Inferences
- [ARITHMETIC] At SRKR, TCS+Infosys+Cognizant+Accenture = 301 + 133 + 333 + 2 = **769 of 2,323 offers (33%)**. Of these, premium-track offers (TCS Digital/Prime, Infosys DSE/SP/L2, GenC Next/Pro) = 69 + 54 + 29 = **152 (20%)**; mass-track = **615**. At LBRCE the premium TCS share is 20 of 30 TCS offers, but TCS is small there; Cognizant (110) and HCL (42) dominate.
- [ARITHMETIC] Premium vs mass salary in the same company at SRKR: TCS 7 vs 3.36 (2.1x); Infosys 7–11 vs 3.6 (1.9–3.1x); Cognizant 6.75 vs 4.1 (1.6x). This is the most concrete "return on upskilling" a counsellor can show — but phrase as published offer levels, never as a promise (CCPA, [PRIOR]).
- [INFERRED] **Demand ranking for coastal-AP B.Tech students (paid):** (1) placement-test prep with DSA/coding aimed at Digital/Prime/DSE/GenC Next; (2) Java or Python full stack with a deployable project (matches the "live projects" preference [PRIOR] and the full-stack job titles in the sheets); (3) data analytics / AI-tools add-on; (4) cloud/cybersecurity/testing as niche electives. GATE prep is a separate, smaller segment (not researched here — no sourced regional data).
- [INFERRED] Non-IT branches (ECE/EEE/MECH/CIVIL) are a large share of placed students (SRKR: 657 of 1,339) and are mostly placed into 2–3.6 LPA core/trainee roles; some also take TCS Ninja / GenC (ECE rows appear in those tables). A "switch to IT" aptitude+coding course is a plausible second market, but price sensitivity is likely higher.

### Gaps
- Only two college-published company-wise sheets read (SRKR, LBRCE). PVPSIT, VRSEC/SAHE, RVR&JC, KL, VFSTR company-wise tables were not extracted.
- No primary TCS/Infosys/Cognizant/Wipro/Accenture/Capgemini/HCL test syllabi (all prep-site descriptions). Wipro (Elite NTH) and Capgemini test content not researched.
- No survey of what coastal-AP students themselves say they want to learn; regional demand is inferred from recruiter tables and national surveys.

## Q2. Placement strength vs likely demand for paid upskilling

### Takeaway
Use **two axes**: placement rate (anxiety) and median/tier (ability to pay and appetite for premium tracks). High-rate, ~4.5 LPA colleges (PVPSIT, VRSEC, LBRCE, SRKR-type) have strong mass placement already — their students' unmet need is the **Ninja→Digital upgrade**. Low-rate colleges (MIC 52.6% @ 2.4 LPA, Dhanekula 35.9%, NRI Guntur 29%) have the highest anxiety but likely lowest ability to pay; lead with an entry-priced aptitude+coding "get placed" module.

### Cited Findings
- [PRIOR] PVPSIT 96.2% @ 4.5 LPA; VRSEC 84.8% @ 4.6; Bapatla 83.3% @ 4.0; SRKR 58.4% @ 3.5 (batch 2025, Careers360/NIRF); MIC 52.6% @ 2.4; Dhanekula 35.9% (2024); NRI Guntur 29.2% @ 3.25; KL 71.6% @ 8.68; VFSTR 41.1% @ 6.75 with 869 of 1,757 going to higher studies — `BTech colleges placements 2024 to 2026.md`.
- [SOURCED] SRKR's own sheet for 2025-26 shows 1,339 students placed (vs 977 placed for batch 2025 on Careers360) and states 86.8%+ placements on its T&P page — [SRKR 2025-26 PDF](https://www.srkrec.ac.in/assets/documents/placements/2025-26.pdf); [SRKR placements page](https://srkrec.edu.in/placements_drives.php) (search extract). Different batches/definitions, so not directly comparable.
- [SOURCED] Unstop 2026 (as reported): 73% of UG students expect ≥ ₹5 LPA but only ~40% achieve it — [inkl/report](https://www.inkl.com/news/73-pc-ug-students-expect-rs-5-lpa-only-40-pc-achieve-it-report).
- [SOURCED] India's top-5 IT firms reduced headcount by 6,981 in FY26 while TCS hired ~40,000 freshers; Wipro says AI skills are scarce and it "trains and hires" freshers — search-result summary of [newsbytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/indian-it-firms-hire-more-freshers-cut-mid-level-roles/tldr) and [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/india-incs-fresher-hiring-sees-steep-drop-amid-ai-adoption) (snippet-level; not fetched).

### Inferences
- [INFERRED] Segment matrix (for setting conversion k-multipliers per college type in the existing formula):

| Segment | Examples | Anxiety | Ability to pay | Likely paid demand |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 universities (median 6.4–8.7) | KL, VFSTR, SRM-AP, VIT-AP | Low–medium | Highest | Niche: advanced AI/GenAI project, product-company DSA; many go to higher studies (VFSTR) → GATE/GRE not IT |
| High-rate autonomous (80–96%, 4–4.6 LPA) | PVPSIT, VRSEC, Bapatla, LBRCE, Vignan's Lara, NRI Agiripalli | Medium (placed, but at 3.4–4.5) | Medium | **Upgrade to premium tracks** (Digital/Prime/DSE/GenC Next) |
| Mid-rate autonomous (55–67%) | SRKR, RVR&JC, VVIT, KKR&KSR, Gudlavalleru, Sasi, Sri Vasavi, Swarnandhra | High for the unplaced ~35–45% | Medium | Both: placement-test prep for unplaced + upgrade for top quartile |
| Low-rate / low-median / undisclosed | MIC, Dhanekula, NRI Guntur, ALIET, and the 80 non-disclosing Tier 3 colleges | Highest | Lowest | Entry-priced aptitude+coding + mock-interview module; parent-facing |

- [INFERRED] "Low placement → more paid demand" is not safe on its own: the free APSSDC/APSCHE/college-CRT layer is aimed exactly at these colleges, and ₹3,000–5,000 is the observed comfort zone [PRIOR]. Expect higher interest but lower price-point and lower conversion per lead.
- [INFERRED] For high-rate colleges, the right pitch is not "get placed" (they mostly do) but "get the 7 LPA offer instead of the 3.36 LPA one", using the college's own published table as evidence.

## Q3. Free/cheap competing offers and paid competitors' price points

### Takeaway
Free supply is large and state-backed (APSSDC–Oracle 4 lakh, APSSDC–Microsoft 10,000 in rural colleges, APSCHE–EduSkills/SmartInternz virtual internships, NPTEL, IIT SWAYAM+ at ₹500), and APSSDC's paid internship is only ₹1,000. Paid local competitors price long full-stack programmes at **₹20,000 (Naresh IT online) to ₹50,000 (Codegnan Vijayawada offline) to ₹49,000–60,000 (NxtWave)**, with short single-skill courses at ~₹10,000. Tech Drona should not compete with free certificates; it should sell outcome-specific, project- and test-oriented coaching at a price well below the ₹20k–60k bootcamps.

### Inferences
- [INFERRED] Price ladder in the market: free (state/NPTEL/APSSDC) → ₹500–5,000 (SWAYAM+, NIELIT, APSSDC ₹1,000, short courses [PRIOR]) → ₹10,000 (single-language classroom courses) → ₹20,000–60,000 (6-month full-stack bootcamps). Tech Drona has room at **₹3,000–10,000 for a 4–8 week, outcome-specific module** (e.g. "NQT Advanced + Digital interview" or "full-stack capstone in 6 weeks"), positioned as "complements your free APSSDC/NPTEL certificate with the coding test and project recruiters actually check".
- [INFERRED] Do not sell "AI/cloud certification" as the headline: Oracle/Microsoft/APSSDC give these free to the same students. Sell *applied* AI-tool use inside a project or interview prep.
- [INFERRED] The APSCHE 360-h internship (if confirmed) competes for final-years' Jan–Apr time but is also a host opportunity [PRIOR]; a credit-bearing "project internship" wrapper may beat a plain course for 4-2 students.

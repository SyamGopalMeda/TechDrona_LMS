# Student-community and outreach channels: Krishna / NTR / Guntur / Palnadu / Bapatla / West Godavari / Eluru

Research date: 7 Nov 2026. Scope: the 45 operational colleges with the largest IT-related intake (it_core + it_allied) in `portal/data/techdrona.db`, which was read only. The per-college data is in `social_communities.csv`, with one row per college in DB intake order.

Method: social links were read from each college's official website (home page, footer or JS bundle), backed up by web search, gdg.community.dev, IEEE vTools and official event pages. No private individuals' names or phone numbers are recorded. Where an event page lists student coordinators, only the role is noted.

## Headline caveats
- **Follower counts and latest-post dates are blank for all 45 colleges.** Instagram and LinkedIn profile pages could not be read without logging in, and no search snippet showed a count we could trust. Following the brief, none were guessed. A 30-minute manual pass in a logged-in browser would fill these in.
- **Coverage:** Instagram was found for 22 of 45 colleges and LinkedIn for 21. Confidence is high for 17 colleges, medium for 14 and low for 14. Most low-confidence rows are colleges whose websites refused automated fetches, returned 403/406 or sat behind Cloudflare: KITS Guntur, VNITSW, Sasi, Bapatla EC, St. Mary's, KITS Akshar, DIET, SACET and VITW. Two other low rows had no usable links: MLWEC shows a "Coming Soon" page, and NRIIT's footer icons are empty.
- **Some links are not clean college accounts:**
  - These LinkedIn URLs are personal-profile style (`/in/`), not school pages: Swarnandhra, CIET, SVECW/VWU, PSCMR and SRKIT.
  - Sri Vasavi's footer Instagram is its GDG on Campus account (@gdsc.svec).
  - The Vikas Nunna accounts are shared across the Vikas group.
  - The Chalapathi University LinkedIn and Facebook pages carry the names of the older CIET/CIT colleges.
  - CIET's Instagram is spelled `_ciet_offical`, exactly as the site links it.
- **2027 fest dates are not yet published anywhere.** The "likely" months below are taken from the 2025-26 editions.

## Colleges with the most active digital presence
Ranked by evidence: all four official channels linked from the site, plus active chapters and named recurring fests.

GDG on Campus KL has **2,246 members** (seen 7 Nov 2026), the largest student developer community in the region, and is very active (Cloud and SLM workshops Sep–Oct 2026). Fests: Samyak (techno-management) and Surabhi (cultural, ~Mar).

## Region-wide channels
**Biggest single opening:** an APSCHE mandate requires a **360-hour, 12-credit internship for final-year UG students from 16 Nov 2026**. For B.Tech that is the 8th semester, roughly Jan–Apr 2027. Industry partners could register on an "Institute-Industry Connect" portal from 4 Nov 2026. Tech Drona should register as an internship host. Source: GKToday, 4 Nov 2026. The official APSCHE circular and portal URL were not found, so check apsche.ap.gov.in.

**Telegram / WhatsApp.** None of these are official university channels; all are run by third parties.
- "JNTUK Fast Updates" WhatsApp Channel (whatsapp.com/channel/0029VaJSYpxIXnlhlRpTOd2Q): **~125K followers, seen 7 Nov 2026**. Run by jntufastupdates.com. This is the largest JNTUK student reach found, and a candidate for a paid promotion.
- Telegram @jntukkd ("JNTU-KAKINADA", universityupdates.in network): **18.1K subscribers, seen 7 Nov 2026**. The same operator runs instagram.com/university_updates (follower count not seen).
- Telegram @jntugurajada: 715 subscribers. "JNTUK Updates" WhatsApp channel: ~3.6K (search snippet only, unverified).
- APSSDC Telegram @skillsITemployers ("Team APSSDC – Engineering & IT Skills"): **7.17K subscribers, seen 7 Nov 2026**. It offers free state Python, ML and GenAI programmes, which compete with Tech Drona. The older channel @APSKILLENGG exists, but its count was not seen.
- No ANU-specific channel was found, only generic results aggregators.

**Instagram meme/news pages.** No AP-, JNTUK- or engineering-specific page with a verifiable follower count was found. The general Telugu meme pages (@lite_ba ~438K and @meme_raaja ~393K per the Qoruz tracker; Fukkard ~562K by the founder's own claim) are not targeted at students. Searching in the Instagram app is needed. College-level confession and meme pages also exist but were not catalogued.

**AP government skill programmes:**
- APSSDC Naipunyam portal (naipunyam.ap.gov.in): its summer internship for III/IV-year students ran in 2025.
- APSCHE x SmartInternz virtual internship (apsche.smartinternz.com).
- AICTE summer internship (Apr–May).
- APSSDC works through MoUs with each college. Every college has an **APSSDC coordinator / SPOC (role) in its T&P cell**, which is a natural first contact.

**Communities and meetups:**
- **GDG Vijayawada** (gdg.community.dev/gdg-vijayawada): **2,619 members, seen 7 Nov 2026**. Holds DevFest at IIIT Nuzvid in Oct–Dec and offers free speaker and sponsor slots.
- GDG on Campus chapters at KL (2,246), PVPSIT (274), VRSEC, SVECW, Sri Vasavi and KITS Guntur.
- Other groups: null Vijayawada (security), eChai Vijayawada (startups), IEEE Guntur Subsection (student branches at NEC, VVIT and others).
- Not found: GDG Guntur, an AWS user group or a FOSS group in the region.

**Student coordinator roles (existence only, no names):** GDG on Campus Lead in each chapter; IEEE SB Chair and WIE chair; APSSDC / SIH / AQVH college SPOCs (AQVH listed "18 SPOCs for 18 colleges"); fest student coordinators listed on fest pages (e.g. RECKON, PRAKALP); NEC's Social Media Committee listing (PDF on nrtec.in).

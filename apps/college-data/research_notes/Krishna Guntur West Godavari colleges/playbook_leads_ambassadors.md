## Q1. Is "400 paid enrollments per college" feasible?

### Takeaway
- **No college in the three districts can reach 400 *voluntary, individually paid* enrollments by 31 Dec 2026.** At the planning conversion band already in use (0.75 / 1.25 / 2.0% of eligible IT final-years [PRIOR]), 400 would need **20,000–53,000 eligible final-years in a single college**. The largest pool on record is KL University's adjusted 3,174.
- 400 per college becomes plausible only through **institutional deals**: an MoU under which the college mandates a course for a defined cohort, or pays or co-pays for it, typically IT 2nd- and 3rd-years plus final-years. Even then it is plausible only at colleges whose in-scope IT cohort is at least about 800 students (50% coverage, assumed), and only on an academic-semester timeline (Jan–Apr 2027 cohorts), not by 31 Dec.
- **Recommendation:** treat "400 per college" as a **stretch target for an institutional-MoU track** at about 10–14 large colleges in types (a) and (b). Keep the voluntary track on the formula in this note, which gives tens per college, not hundreds. Report the two tracks separately: an institution-paid seat is not the same funnel as an individually paid sale.

### Inferences
- [ARITHMETIC + ASSUMED] **Voluntary ceiling.** Even with every IT year counted, the 400 test fails:
  - Final-years at 2%, 3rd-years at 1%, 2nd-years at 0.5%, all years about 800 each: about 28 paid.
  - KL's largest pool (about 3,174 final-years plus about 3,000 per lower year): about 63 + 30 + 15 ≈ 108 at max.
  - **No college reaches 400 voluntarily**, even at max and even over a full year, unless measured conversion beats the planning band 3–4×.
- [INFERRED] **Institutional penetration is a different regime.** Free or mandated programmes reach 80%+ of a cohort (KSRMCE) or a fixed quota of about 250 per college (APSSDC). The student-pay premium tier in vendor MoUs is selective, at about 100 per college per year for Pega/TalentSprint. 400 *paid* per college therefore means either:
  1. the college pays or co-pays for a mandated cohort (an institution-paid batch), or
  2. a multi-year voluntary programme at a very large university.

  Option 2 does not reach 400 on the arithmetic above.
- [ASSUMED] **Planning coverage for an institutional MoU:** 30% / 50% / 80% of the in-scope cohort for min / avg / max. The 80% sits below the KSRMCE 84%, which was free. This must be validated with the first signed MoU.
- **Rule (apply per college):**

| Model | When feasible | How 400 is reached |
|---|---|---|
| A. Voluntary, individually paid (current track) | Every college, but yields tens, not hundreds | Final-years plus 3rd- and 2nd-years at the formula rates below |
| B. Institutional MoU (college pays or co-pays; whole cohort; credit or value-added course) | Type (a) or (b) college, **in-scope IT cohort ≥ 800** (400 ÷ 0.5) and a willing management | e.g. all IT 3rd-years plus 2nd-years in a 30–60 h value-added or "honours/minor" course, invoiced to the college |
| C. Hybrid (free base for all, premium paid tier) | Most type (a)/(b)/(c) colleges | Free complementary sessions build the funnel; paid tier at about 100/college/yr (Pega analogue); voluntary formula applies |
| Not feasible (any model) | In-scope IT cohort < 800, or type (c)/(d) colleges with no management budget | Cap at the formula max; do not set 400 |

- [INFERRED] **Size bands for the 400 test** (IT students in years 2–4, i.e. the 2023, 2024 and 2025 entry batches):

| In-scope IT students (years 2–4) | 400 via model B | Typical colleges |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 2,400 | Feasible at avg coverage on one or two years only | KL, VFSTR, VIT-AP, SRM-AP |
| 800–2,399 | Feasible only if the MoU covers all three years at ≥ 50% | Big autonomous JNTUK colleges |
| < 800 | Not feasible; use the formula max | Most type (c) and (d) colleges |

  The college lists in the second and third columns are indicative and must be checked with the branch CSVs.
- [INFERRED] **Timing.** Institutional MoUs need management, academic council or budget approval. 4-1 exams run until 27 Nov, and the APSCHE 360-h internship (if confirmed) starts 16 Nov. A model B cohort realistically starts with the **even semester (Jan 2027)**. It should be reported as a 2027 pipeline, not counted toward 31 Dec 2026.

### Gaps
- No primary source gives **paid per-college penetration** for TalentSprint, Smart Interviews, ExcelR, Ethnus or FACE CRT, and no **per-student fee in any institution-paid CRT MoU**. The FACE Prep MoU on Scribd mentions "payment terms for placements secured" but its amounts are hidden ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/560280932/CTPMS-MoU-New-KR)).

## Q2. Finding and preparing College Leads without collecting personal data

### Takeaway
Find candidates **through institutions, not data**: TPO, HOD or club-faculty nominations, plus the student roles that colleges publish themselves (fest event coordinators, ACM/CSI/IEEE/GDG chapter office-bearers, hackathon organisers). Then **ask the institution to forward an application link**, so that the candidate opts in. Score each applicant on six criteria, run a 7-day trial task, and certify through a 4-module onboarding with a compliance exam before any selling.

### Cited findings
- [SOURCED] Colleges publish student-coordinator roles on official pages:
  - **PVP Siddhartha**'s SITAR 2K26 fest pages list "Student Coordinators for this Event" by department, for CSE, IT and ECE ([PVPSIT SITAR IT](https://www.pvpsiddhartha.ac.in/techfest26/it.html), [CSE](https://www.pvpsiddhartha.ac.in/techfest26/cse.html)).
  - **SAHE / VR Siddhartha** reports on ACM-chapter events such as Code Relay (20 Sep 2025) and a 24-hour hackathon describe the student-coordinator roles ([SAHE events list](https://siddhartha.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Main-Events-List.pdf)).
  - **SRKR** publishes hackathon news, for example the Dev Challenge of 31 Jan–1 Feb 2025 ([SRKR news](https://srkrec.ac.in/news_details.php?id=609)) and HackOverflow ([SRKR TP](https://srkrec.edu.in/files/TP/hackoverflow_2024.pdf)).
  - **RVR&JC** runs AIKSHETRA CodeWarz ([RVR&JC](https://rvrjcce.ac.in/xcsm/aikshetra2K25/codewarz/)).
  - Some of these pages include students' personal phone numbers. **Do not harvest them.**
- [SOURCED] Google's student-club lead selection is a useful template:
  - Eligibility includes **at least one year left until graduation**.
  - The application covers technical skills, leadership or event-organising history and a vision for the campus.
  - There is a short self-intro **video** (90 seconds in the GDSC era), then shortlisting and an interview. Some campuses run a nomination event ([Google GDSC leads](https://developers.google.cn/community/gdsc/leads?hl=en); [DEV community account](https://dev.to/gdg-geu/become-a-gdg-on-campus-organizer-your-complete-selection-journey-4plj); [Galgotias nomination event](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-on-campus-galgotias-college-of-engineering-technology-greater-noida-india-presents-leadership-leap-gdg-on-campus-organizer-nomination/)).
- [SOURCED] Makai's ambassador onboarding guide sets out 5 stages: Understand, Demonstrate, Execute, Certify and Reinforce. Key points:
  - **7–10 business days** from hire to deployment.
  - Role-plays: a 15–20 second pitch, and objection responses.
  - A **certification gate** of a written claims exam plus an oral demo before independent work, with remediation for anyone who fails.
  - Weekly 5-minute briefings and unannounced field audits ([Makai onboarding guide](https://www.makaiinc.com/resources/the-complete-student-brand-ambassador-onboarding-guide)).

  This guide is US consumer-brand material. The structure transfers; the specific numbers may not.
- [SOURCED] **Compliance content for training:**
  - **CCPA "Prevention of Misleading Advertisement in Coaching Sector" (13 Nov 2024)** prohibits:
    - "100% selection" or "100% job" claims, and guaranteed job security or salary increases;
    - false claims about course duration, faculty, **fees and refund policy**;
    - use of successful candidates' names, photos or testimonials **without written consent obtained after selection**.

    It also requires prominent disclaimers and accurate course details ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/centre-issues-coaching-centres-guidelines-jee-neet-upsc-cuet-2025-crack-down-on-misleading-ads); [PIB draft notice](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1994532)). "Coaching" includes "education, guidance … tuition", so a paid IT course sold to students should be treated as covered [INFERRED].
  - **Meta WhatsApp:** businesses must obtain opt-in before messaging. The person must be clearly told they are opting in to messages from *that business*. Meta allows general consent, but a WhatsApp-specific opt-in is recommended ([Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in); [Braze](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/whatsapp-evolutions-2025-three-key-updates.md)).
  - **TRAI TCCCPR, 2nd amendment (12 Feb 2025):** promotional calls must come from the **140 series**, transactional and service calls from 1600, and **10-digit numbers are barred for telemarketing**. The definition of promotional communication was widened to stop "service" disguise ([TRAI PR 11/2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf); [JSA](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/JSA-Prism-Telecommunications_February-2025_TCCCPR-amendments.Final_.pdf)). Penalty figures differ between sources ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/telcos-disappointed-with-new-trai-rules-to-curb-spam-calls-heres-why)).
  - **ASCI influencer guidelines:** any **material connection**, cash or not, must be disclosed with a label such as "Ad", "Sponsored", "Collaboration" or "Partnership". A platform's own toggle is not enough, and the advertiser must make sure its promoters comply ([Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/thought-leaderships/ASCI-releases-influencer-advertising-guidelines-for-digital-media-Effective-14-June-21-onward); [ASCI 2025 release](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Press-Release-Influencer-Disclosure-Guidelines-ASCI-Report.pdf)). A commission-earning ambassador's Instagram post is a material connection [INFERRED].
  - **DPDP Rules 2025** were notified 13/14 Nov 2025. The notice, consent, retention and breach duties apply **18 months later (about mid-May 2027)**, and the SPDI Rules 2011 continue meanwhile ([PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)).

### Inferences: sourcing channels, in order [ASSUMED]
1. **TPO or placement-cell nomination**: "two final- or pre-final-year students who already coordinate placement drives or CRT".
2. **HOD or club-faculty nomination** from CSE/IT/AI-DS: chapter office-bearers of ACM, CSI, IEEE, GDG on Campus, coding clubs or E-cells.
3. **Official event pages** (fest or hackathon coordinators): use the **role only** as a signal. Pass a generic invitation through the faculty coordinator or the club's official handle. Never cold-message a number lifted from a page.
4. **Inbound application form** (public link on Tech Drona's site and posters approved by the TPO). Applicants supply their own details, with a consent tick box.
5. **Promotion from within:** after week 3, the top ambassador at the college becomes a co-lead or replacement lead.

**Data rule:** Tech Drona stores a candidate's details only after the candidate submits the application form themselves. The form carries a purpose notice, a retention period and withdrawal contact. The scouting list holds *college + role + source URL* only, with no names or numbers.

### Inferences: College Lead evaluation rubric [ASSUMED weights and anchors]

| Criterion | Weight | 1 (weak) | 3 (adequate) | 5 (strong) | How to evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Network reach | 25% | Knows own section only | Known across the branch / year | Office-bearer reaching several branches and years; runs a club group with consented members | Role on official page or nomination; size of the club they run (self-reported, spot-checked) |
| Credibility (faculty and peer trust) | 20% | No faculty referee | One faculty referee | TPO/HOD nominated; trusted with fest money or placement coordination | Nomination letter or email from an official address |
| Sales and communication aptitude | 20% | Reads the script; freezes on objections | Clear pitch; handles 2 of 4 objections | Natural Telugu + English explainer; handles price, parents and "placement guarantee?" honestly | Role-play scored in interview |
| Availability during exams and the sprint | 15% | Final-year in 4-1 exams to 27 Nov with labs pending, or project and internship clash | Some exam blocks; can commit 3–4 h/week | Pre-final year with staggered exams; commits 5–8 h/week after 28 Nov | Exam timetable (DB `exams`) + written commitment |
| Digital presence and tools | 10% | No WhatsApp group admin experience | Uses Sheets/Forms; modest social presence | Runs club handles or communities; comfortable with portal/CRM | Shows a club page they manage (no follower scraping) |
| Prior event organising | 10% | None | Volunteer at a fest | Led a hackathon, workshop or fest track; booked rooms and handled the TPO | Official event page / report naming the role |

- **Score** = Σ(weight × score), on a 1–5 scale. **Appoint** at ≥ 3.8 with no criterion below 2. **Ambassador instead** at 3.0–3.79. **Hold** below 3.0.
- **Tie-breakers:** a pre-final year beats a final year (they continue into 2027 and their exams are staggered), then a candidate whose branch differs from the existing ambassadors.
- **Ambassador rubric:** use the same 6 rows with weights 30 / 15 / 25 / 15 / 10 / 5. Appoint at ≥ 3.0.

**Interview (20 min) [ASSUMED]:**
1. 2 min self-intro (a 60–90 s video can replace it, per the GDSC model).
2. 5 min on "your campus map": which groups, years and branches you can reach, and how.
3. 8 min role-play with the counsellor as a student and as a parent. Required objections:
   - "Is placement guaranteed?" (the correct answer is no: describe the placement-support scope);
   - "₹X is too much";
   - "after exams";
   - "my college already gives certifications".
4. 5 min on the compliance quiz (verbal).

**7-day trial task (unpaid except commission on any sales; no pressure on classmates) [ASSUMED]:**
- Get a written TPO/HOD OK for one 60–90 min session slot, or a club-event tie-in.
- Collect **≥ 25 consented opt-ins** through the official form or QR code only.
- Recruit **≥ 3 ambassador applicants** who apply themselves.
- Submit one 150-word campus plan.

Pass if 3 of the 4 are done. The trial's leading indicators become the lead's first KPIs.

**Onboarding and certification (about 6–7 h over 7–10 days, adapted from Makai's stages) [ASSUMED content]:**

| Module | Hours | Content | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Course and product | 1.5 | Syllabus, live-project demo, batch dates, honest outcomes, the price ladder (early-bird, group-of-3, code discount, 2-part UPI), refund policy text | 10-question quiz ≥ 80% |
| 2. Counselling script and objection handling | 2 | 10–15 min call flow (status → goal → skills gap → fit → logistics → fee → parent check → close), with Telugu and English versions; 8 objection cards | Recorded role-play scored ≥ 3/5 by a DCL |
| 3. Compliance | 1 | CCPA: never "100% placement" or "guaranteed job"; exact fees and refunds; no testimonials or photos without written consent. ASCI: label posts "Ad / Tech Drona Campus Partner". Meta: opt-in before any WhatsApp, no mass-adding to groups. TRAI: no bulk promo calls or SMS from personal numbers; company promotional calls only via registered 140-series or DLT routes. Data: no screenshots or exports of class lists, store nothing outside the portal. Payments: no cash, gateway link only | 15-question exam, **100% on the 5 red-line items**, ≥ 80% overall |
| 4. Tools and attribution | 1 | Portal lead entry, the dedupe check, referral code and payment link, the 21-day ownership rule, the daily huddle format | Enters 3 test leads correctly |
| 5. Shadow | 1–2 | Co-runs one session or 3 counselling calls with a DCL or counsellor | DCL sign-off |

Re-certify on compliance if the course, price or refund policy changes. Send a weekly 5-minute briefing note, the Makai "reinforce" stage.

### Gaps
- There is no Indian, published scoring rubric for student-sales leads. The weights above are judgement.
- The final CCPA text was read through a news summary. Fetch the official PDF from consumeraffairs.nic.in for the training deck.
- The application of DLT/140-series rules to 1:1 peer calls from a student's own phone is not tested in any source. Treat ambassadors' 1:1 calls to people who opted in as interpersonal, and route all scaled calling through Tech Drona's registered lines [ASSUMED].

## Q3. Managing the network

### Takeaway
Keep the earlier blueprint's structure:
- spans of **3–7 ambassadors per College Lead** and 5–7 College Leads per DCL;
- 7- and 14-day inactivity rules;
- gateway-only "paid" status;
- weekly payouts after the refund window.

Add a **lead-specific incentive ladder** that pays the College Lead for the *team's* output (an override plus milestone bonuses), not for personal sales. Hold every leaderboard to **net confirmed** enrollments.

### Cited findings
- [PRIOR] Spans: Alexander Group gives about 8.5 for first-line sales managers, smaller for inexperienced reps. FEMA ICS gives 3–7 with an optimum of 5. Teams of ≤ 7 attain better (Tunguz, correlation only).
- [PRIOR] Inactivity: Makai flags anyone with no verified activity for 2 weeks; reporting compliance fell from 85% to 20% after midterms. Affiliate benchmarks put 25–40% of partners active, or 50–70% when directly recruited.

## Q4. Marketing templates by college type

### Takeaway
One funnel serves all four types:

> Consented opt-in → same-day counselling → seat-hold token or early-bird → full payment within 7 days → confirmation.

The **door, the pitch and the price ladder** differ by type:
- **(a) Deemed universities:** department, club and hackathon access; a complementary, project-led pitch.
- **(b) Big autonomous colleges:** TPO-led CRT and placement tie-in, plus fests.
- **(c) Small-town affiliated colleges:** principal or TPO session, Telugu, parent involvement, the lowest price ladder.
- **(d) Women's colleges:** daytime on-campus sessions, women counsellors, parent-inclusive communication.

## Per-college target formula (for the consolidator)

Inputs come from `<slug>_colleges.csv` / `<slug>_branches.csv`. Use the verified value if present, otherwise the estimated one.

| Input | Meaning |
|---|---|
| F | IT final-year students (2023 entry), `it_final_year` |
| T | IT 3rd-year students (2024 entry intake, × 0.95 retention [ASSUMED]) |
| S | IT 2nd-year students (2025 entry intake, × 0.97 [ASSUMED]) |
| N | Non-IT final-years (optional; counted only if Tech Drona offers a course that suits them) |
| k | College-type multiplier from Q4 (min / avg / max) |
| x | Access factor: 1.0 if a TPO line is verified, 0.8 if office line only, 0.6 if none (consistent with the existing contact factor) |

Voluntary rates by segment ([PRIOR] for F; the rest [ASSUMED]):

| Segment | Min | Avg | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT final-year (F) | 0.75% | 1.25% | 2.0% |
| IT 3rd-year (T), half of F as in the GGU precedent | 0.375% | 0.625% | 1.0% |
| IT 2nd-year (S) | 0.15% | 0.25% | 0.5% |
| Non-IT final-year (N) | 0.2% | 0.35% | 0.6% |

**Voluntary target for each scenario s ∈ {min, avg, max}:**

```
V_s = round( k_s × x × ( r_F,s·F + r_T,s·T + r_S,s·S + r_N,s·N ) )
```

Floors: V_min ≥ 2 for any open college with a contact line.

**Institutional add-on (model B), counted only after a signed MoU and a purchase order:**

```
I_s = c_s × C
```

Here C is the MoU-scope cohort (e.g. T + S), and c_s = 0.30 / 0.50 / 0.80 [ASSUMED]. Before signature, I = 0 in min/avg. Show `I_max` only as a "pipeline" column.

**Totals and the 400 test:**
- Total_s = V_s + I_s.
- 400-per-college feasibility flag:
  - **GREEN** if V_avg ≥ 400. None are expected.
  - **AMBER** if V_max + 0.5 × (F + T + S) ≥ 400 **and** the type is (a) or (b), **and** management is open to an MoU. In practice this means an in-scope IT cohort ≥ about 800.
  - **RED** otherwise. Set the target at Total_s and do not assign 400.

**Worked check [ARITHMETIC; voluntary only, for illustration with T = S = F]:** a type (b) college with F = 1,200 and x = 1:

| Scenario | Calculation | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Min | 1,200 × (0.75 + 0.375 + 0.15)% = 1,200 × 1.275% | 15.3 → 15 |
| Avg | 1,200 × (1.25 + 0.625 + 0.25)% = 1,200 × 2.125% | 25.5 → 26 (round .5 up) |
| Max | 1,200 × (2.0 + 1.0 + 0.5)% = 1,200 × 3.5% | 42 |

That is far from 400, so the college is AMBER only via an MoU.

Note for the 31 Dec 2026 sprint: T and S students are reachable only after their own exams (2-1 classes resume around 7 Dec [PRIOR]). Count T and S at 50% for the 2026 window and at 100% for a Jan–Mar 2027 window [ASSUMED].
